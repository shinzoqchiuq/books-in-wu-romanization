## S-DU YIU-DA-GO SHÜ-SING. 使徒<u>猶大</u>個書信

<sup>1</sup> Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go nu-boh, Yüô-kôh-go hyüong-di Yiu-da, *sia* *sing* peh *Tʽin*-Vu Jing-ming sô tseng-kyʽi, Yiæ-su Kyi-toh sô pao-yiu, sô dziao-læ cü-kwu. <sup>2</sup> Dæn-nyün dz-pe, bing-en, jing-æ, ʽeo-ʽeo s-peh ng-lah.

<sup>1</sup> <u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個奴僕、<u>雅各</u>個兄弟<u>猶大</u>，寫信撥天父神明所尊起、<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>所保佑、所召來主顧。<sup>2</sup> 但願慈悲、平安、仁愛，厚厚賜撥爾拉。

<sup>3</sup> Tsʽing-æ-go *hyüong-di*, ngô kyi-jün ing-ing gying-gying sia sing peh ng-lah, nyi-leng kyiu ah-lah cong-nying go *eng-we* pih kyʽiao-leh sia *liang-kyü* kyʽün ng-lah yüong cʽih-lih pao-siu, dzong-zin sô djün-ziu sing-du go dao-li. <sup>4</sup> Ing-we yiu kyi-go nying kô læ jih-kyiao, z yü-sin ding-kwe iao ziu keh ying-vah go; gyi-lah z feh gyin-sing, *kyʽi-diao* ah-lah Jing-ming-go eng-we, kyʽi tso ying-lön z-ken, zing-ts-ü feh tsiao-jing doh-ih-we Cü-tsæ Jing-ming, teng ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh. <sup>5</sup> *Sô-yi* yiu ih-yiang z-ken, se-tsih ng-lah yi-kying ming-bah, ngô iao tsæ di-sing ng-lah, ziu-z Cü kyi-kying kyiu pah-sing cʽih Yiæ-gyih di-fông, ʽeo-deo yi we djü-mih keh-sing feh siang-sing cü-kwu. <sup>6</sup> Wa-yiu keh-sing *tʽin-s* feh siu-djü peng-læ-go tsih-veng, li-kʽæ gyi-zi-go di-we, Cü pô üong-yün feh-dön-go lin-diao læ heh-en li-hyiang kwun-ah gyi teng-tao sing-pʽun-go do nyih-ts læ; <sup>7</sup> yi-ziang Sô-to-mô, Ngo-mo-lah teng gyi dön-kyʽün kôh zing-li, yia z-ka tso ying-lön z-ken, lin gyi-cʽih-gyi-yiang-go s-yüôh, gyi-lah læ-tih ziu üong-yün ho-siao-go ying-vah z ming-ming hyin-cʽih pông-yiang peh ah-lah kʽen. <sup>8</sup> Se-z ka wô, keh-sing nying wa-we *ziang* tso-mông *ka*, me-u gyi-go kyi-sing, kyʽing-mæn yiu gyün-ping, sih-doh yiu yüong-wô cü-kwu. <sup>9</sup> Dæn-z ting-do *tʽin-s* Mi-kyüô-leh teng mo-kwe læ-tih nyi-leng Mo-si-go s-siu yia feh-ken djong-yin djong-nyü ze-joh gyi; tæn-tsih <sup>\[1\]</sup>wô, “Cü tsiang-læ we tsah-vah ng gyi.” <sup>10</sup> Dæn-z keh-pæn nying we pông-hwe gyi sô feh hyiao-teh go z-ken; ziu-z gyi sang-dzing, ziang m li-sing go cong-sang ka, sô hyiao-teh go z-ken, lin keh-sing *u-sen* tu we zi u-wæn zi. <sup>11</sup> Gyi-lah yiu ʽo-se de, ing-we z tseo Kæ-ing keh-da lu, we-leh tʽen-dzæ hyüih-sing ʽôh Pô-læn-go w̆ang-li, ziu mih-vông læ Ko-lah-go tsʽao-lön li-hyiang.

<sup>3</sup> 親愛個兄弟，我既然殷殷勤勤寫信撥爾拉，議論救阿拉衆人個恩惠必巧勒寫兩句勸爾拉用出力保守，從前所傳授聖徒個道理。<sup>4</sup> 因爲有幾個人假來入教，是預先定規要受箇刑罰個；其拉是弗虔心，棄掉阿拉神明個恩惠，去做淫亂事幹，甚至於弗招認獨一位主宰神明，等阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>。<sup>5</sup> 所以有一樣事幹，雖即爾拉已經明白，我要再提醒爾拉，就是主既經救百姓出<u>埃及</u>地方，後頭又會除滅箇星弗相信主顧。<sup>6</sup> 還有箇星天使弗守住本來個職份，離開其自個地位，主把永遠弗斷個鏈條來黑暗裏向管押其等到審判個大日子來。<sup>7</sup> 又像<u>所多瑪</u>、<u>蛾摩拉</u>等其團圈各城裏，也是介做淫亂事幹，連奇出奇樣個私慾，其拉來的受永遠火燒個刑罰是明明顯出榜樣撥阿拉看。<sup>8</sup> 雖是介話，箇星人還會像做夢介，黴污其個肌身，輕慢有權柄，褻瀆有榮華主顧。<sup>9</sup> 但是頂大天使<u>米迦勒</u>等魔鬼來的議論<u>摩西</u>個屍首也弗敢重言重語罪辱其；單只話<sup>\[1\]</sup>，「主將來會責罰爾其。」<sup>10</sup> 但是箇班人會謗毀其所弗曉得個事幹；就是其生成，像嘸理性個衆生介，所曉得個事幹，連箇星烏碎都會自污環自。<sup>11</sup> 其拉有禍祟了，因爲是走<u>該隱</u>箇埭路，爲了貪財血心學<u>巴蘭</u>個橫禮，就滅亡來<u>哥拉</u>個吵亂裏向。

<sup>\[1\] 此處原書錯印爲 wo</sup>

<sup>12</sup> Keh-sing nying ʽao-vu gyi-dæn teng ng-lah dong-zih kyʽüoh-væn, tsih-kwu pao zi-go du-bi, keh-z ng-lah jing-æ tsiu-yin-go yüô-tin; gyi kʽo-pi z m-yü-go yüing be fong sô pʽiao-dong go; yi kʽo-pi tsʽiu-tʽin *m-yih* feh sang ko-ts go jü, si-ko yi-si, lin keng-cü tu be bah-diao; <sup>13</sup> Yi kʽo-pi hæ-li hyüong-hyin-go po-lông hyin-cʽih gyi-zi-go tsʽiu-tʽæ; yi kʽo-pi yi-ko-go liu-sing, yiu üong-yün heh-en-go *u-sen* teng gyi kæn. <sup>14</sup> Üô-tông di-tsʽih-dæ-go seng-ts Yi-nôh yü-sin ts-tin keh-pæn nying, z-ka wô, <sup>15</sup> “Cü teng gyi vu-tsʽin da-væn-go sing-du ziu we læ sing-pʽun cong-nying, pô keh-sing feh gyin-sing-go ze-nying sô tso ih-tsʽih feh gyin-sing-go z-ken, teng gyi sô kông ih-tsʽih pông-hwe Cü go ôh-wô kyʽi tsah-vah væn-pah feh gyin-sing cü-kwu.”

<sup>12</sup> 箇星人毫無忌憚等爾拉同席喫飯，只顧飽自個肚皮，箇是爾拉仁愛酒筵個瑕點；其可比是嘸雨個雲被風所飄動個；又可比秋天嘸葉弗生果子個樹，死過又死，連根株都被拔掉。<sup>13</sup> 又可比海裏兇險個波浪顯出其自個醜態；又可比移過個流星，有永遠黑暗個烏碎等其間。<sup>14</sup> <u>亞當</u>第七代個孫子<u>以諾</u>預先指點箇班人，是介話，<sup>15</sup> 「主等其無千大萬個聖徒就會來審判衆人，把箇星弗虔心個罪人所做一切弗虔心個事幹，等其所講一切謗毀主個惡話去責罰凡百弗虔心主顧。」

<sup>16</sup> Keh-pæn nying m-neh tsôh-sing, z-kʽeh ün-tʽin ʽeng-di, ʽeo zi-go s-yüôh ka tso; Gyi-lah kʽeo-li kông-go z do-wô, we-leh dzæ-li tsʽeo nying-kô-go cʽü. <sup>17</sup> Tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di, ng-lah kæ kyi-teh ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go s-du yü-sin sô kông go z-ka wô, <sup>18</sup> Meh-dæ-go z-shü pih-yiu kyʽing-mæn-go cü-kwu cʽih-læ ʽeo zi feh gyin-sing-go s-yüôh ka tso. <sup>19</sup> Keh-pæn nying tsih-yiu s-yüôh m̆-teh ling-sing, sô-yi iao teng kong-we feng-kʽæ.

<sup>16</sup> 箇班人嘸得作聲，時刻怨天恨地，候自個私慾介做；其拉口裏講個是大話，爲了財利湊人家個趣。<sup>17</sup> 親愛個兄弟，爾拉該記得阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個使徒預先所講個是介話，<sup>18</sup> 末代個時世必有輕慢個主顧出來候自弗虔心個私慾介做。<sup>19</sup> 箇班人只有私慾嘸得靈性，所以要等公會分開。

<sup>20, 21</sup> Ng-lah tsʽing-æ-go *hyüong-di*, *kʽao-djôh* Jing-ming-go jing-æ yüong zi kwu-djôh zi, pô ng-lah sô siang-sing keh sing-jün-go *dao-li*, dzing-dziu zi-go teh-kyʽi, kʽao-djôh Sing-Ling yüong z-djông tao-kao, z-djông siang-vông ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go dz-pe hao teh djôh üong-yün weh-ming. <sup>22</sup> Tsih-z gyi-cong iao feng-pih hao-tæ, yiu-sing yüong kwʽun-shü gyi; <sup>23</sup> yiu-sing iao-ziang kah-ho te-cʽih ka, kyʽih-hyin kyʽih-hoh kyʽi kyiu gyi, ziu-z gyi be s-yüôh tang tsih-le go i-zông yia tu iao kʽeh-tsʽeng gyi.

<sup>20, 21</sup> 爾拉親愛個兄弟，靠着神明個仁愛用自顧着自，把爾拉所相信箇聖善個道理，成就自個德氣，靠着聖靈用時常禱告，時常想望阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個慈悲好得着永遠活命。<sup>22</sup> 只是其中要分別好歹，有星用寬恕其；<sup>23</sup> 有星要像隔火𠡒出介，喫險喫𢞕去救其，就是其被私慾打漬洡個衣裳也都要刻忖其。

<sup>24, 25</sup> Dæn-nyün yüong-wô, w̆e-nyin, neng-ken, gyün-ping, dzong yin-dzæ ih-dzih tao shü-shü dæ-dæ kwe peh keh doh ih-we tsʽong-ming-go Jing-ming ah-lah-go Kyiu-Cü. Gyi neng-keo pao-wu ah-lah ve pæn-tih, s-teh ah-lah m pʽi-bing hao kwʽa-kwʽa weh-weh lih læ Gyi-zi yüong-wô-go min-zin. Üô-meng.

<sup>24, 25</sup> 但願榮華、威嚴、能幹、權柄，從現在一直到世世代代歸撥箇獨一位聰明個神明阿拉個救主。其能夠保護阿拉𣍐𢲔跌，使得阿拉嘸批評好快快活活立來其自榮華個面前。亞們。
