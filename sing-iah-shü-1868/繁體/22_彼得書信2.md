## S-DU PE-TEH-GO SHÜ-SING. DI-NYI-FONG. 使徒<u>彼得</u>個書信 第二封

### 2P. I.

Tso Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go nu-boh teng s-du go, Si-meng Pe-teh sia sing peh keh-sing teng ah-lah dô-kô teh-djôh ʽong-eng hao siang-sing <sup>\[1\]</sup>Jing-ming, ah-lah Kyiu-cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go dao-yi cü-kwu. <sup>2</sup> Dæn-nyün ng-lah sih-teh Jing-ming teng ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su. we kô-be teh-djôh eng-we teng bing-en.

做<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個奴僕等使徒個，<u>西門·彼得</u>寫信撥箇星等阿拉大家得着鴻恩好相信神明<sup>\[1\]</sup>、阿拉救主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個道義主顧。<sup>2</sup> 但願爾拉識得神明等阿拉主<u>耶穌</u>，會加倍得着恩惠等平安。

<sup>\[1\] *ʽÔh* *wô*, ah-lah Jing-ming teng Kyiu-cü. 或話，阿拉神明等救主。（原註）</sup>

<sup>3</sup> Jing-ming yüong zi-go neng-ken s-peh ah-lah ih-tsʽih joh-ü weh-ming teng gyin-dzing go z-ken; s-teh ah-lah hao-hyiao-teh keh-we pô yüong-wô-go teh-kyʽi dziao ah-lah læ go Cü-kwu. <sup>4</sup> Wa-yiu s-peh ah-lah hyü-to ting pao-pe-go ing-hyü, s-teh ng-lah we-leh keh-sing ing-hyü neng-keo kyʽi-diao shü-dzoh-go s-yüôh, teh-djôh Jing-ming-go sing-dzing.

<sup>3</sup> 神明用自個能幹賜撥阿拉一切屬於活命等虔誠個事幹；使得阿拉好曉得箇位把榮華個德氣召阿拉來個主顧。<sup>4</sup> 還有賜撥阿拉許多頂寶貝個應許，使得爾拉爲了箇星應許能夠棄掉世俗個私慾，得着神明個性情。

<sup>5</sup> Sô-yi yüong dzing-sing-gyih-lih ü ng-lah-go siang-sing, kô-zông tæn-liang; tæn-liang, kô-zông cü-sih; <sup>\[1\]</sup><sup>6</sup> cü-sih, kô-zông tsih-cü; tsih-cü, kô-zông jing-næ; jing-næ, kô-zông gyin-dzing; <sup>7</sup> gyin-dzing, kô-zông hyüong-di-go tsʽing-gying; hyüong-di-go tsʽing-gying, kô-zông jing-æ. <sup>8</sup> Keh-sing ʽang-we ziah læ ng-lah sing-zông we to-kyʽi-læ, ziu hao tso bing-kyü ng-lah sih-teh ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh tsæ-ve yiæ-dæ, tsæ-ve m-dao-dzing. <sup>9</sup> M-teh keh-sing ʽang-we cü-kwu z ziang hah-ts, feh-neng-keo dza kʽen-kyin, z mông-kyi gyi zin-deo-go ze-nyih yi-kying gyiang ken-zing de. <sup>10</sup> Sô-yi, hyüong-di, yüong yü-kô zông-kying kyʽi kʽao-jih ng-lah be dziao-læ be kæn-shün go eng-we: ng-lah z-djông tso keh-sing z-ken, tsæ-ve feh-dzing-kong. <sup>11</sup> Z-ka tso-fah, ziu we kʽæ peh ng ih-da kwʽun-tsʽiah-go lu, hao tseo-tsing ah-lah Kyiu-Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go üong-yün koh li-hyiang kyʽi.

<sup>5</sup> 所以用盡心竭力於爾拉個相信，加上膽量；膽量，加上知識；<sup>6</sup><sup>\[1\]</sup> 知識，加上節制；節制，加上忍耐；忍耐，加上虔誠；<sup>7</sup> 虔誠，加上兄弟個親近；兄弟個親近，加上仁愛。<sup>8</sup> 箇星行爲若來爾拉身上會多起來，就好做憑據爾拉識得阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>再𣍐懈怠、再𣍐嘸陶成。<sup>9</sup> 嘸得箇星行爲主顧是像瞎子，弗能夠咋看見，是忘記其前頭個罪孽已經滰乾淨了。<sup>10</sup> 所以，兄弟，用愈加上緊去靠實爾拉被召來被揀選個恩惠。爾拉時常做箇星事幹，再𣍐弗成功。<sup>11</sup> 是介做法，就會開撥爾一埭寬綽個路，好走進阿拉救主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個永遠國裏向去。

<sup>\[1\] 原書此處缺編號 6</sup>

<sup>12</sup> Keh-go dao-li, ng-lah yin-dzæ se-jün ming-bah, kyin-kwu siang-sing, ngô yia feh-hao yiæ-dæ, kæ z-djông pô keh-sing z-ken di-sing ng-lah. <sup>13</sup> Ngô tsʽeng-tsʽeng ngô wa deng-læ keh-go tsiang-bong li-hyiang z ing-kæ di-sing ng-lah, kyih-dong ng-lah: <sup>14</sup> ing-we hyiao-teh ngô kwʽa-iao tʽeh-cʽih ngô-go tsiang-bong, ziang ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh yi-kying tin-hyiang ngô dao. <sup>15</sup> Ngô yia pih-iao dzing-sing-gyih-lih, s-teh ng-lah, ngô kyʽü-shü ts-ʽeo, neng-keo z-kʽeh kyi-teh keh-sing z-ken.

<sup>12</sup> 箇個道理，爾拉現在雖然明白，堅固相信，我也弗好懈怠，該時常把箇星事幹提醒爾拉。<sup>13</sup> 我忖忖我還庉來箇個帳篷裏向是應該提醒爾拉，激動爾拉。<sup>14</sup> 因爲曉得我快要脫出我個帳篷，像阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>已經點向我道。<sup>15</sup> 我也必要盡心竭力，使得爾拉，我去世之後，能夠時刻記得箇星事幹。

<sup>16</sup> Ah-lah yi-kying tʽong-cü ng ah-lah Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh tsih-công gyün-ping tsæ læ-go z-ken, bing-feh-z yüong kwæ-kyʽiao nyiah-zao-go shih-wô, z tsʽing-ngæn kʽen-kyin Gyi-go w̆e-nyin; <sup>17</sup> ziu-z dzong keh kwông-ming-go yüong-wô li-hyiang yiu ka ih-go sing-ing teng Gyi wô, “Keh z Ngô tsʽing-æ-go Ng-ts, Ngô ting teh-i go” \[Mt. 17. 5\]: keh-go z-ʽeo *Tʽin*-Vu Jing-ming s-peh Gyi tʽi-min teng yüong-wô. <sup>18</sup> Keh dzong tʽin læ go sing-ing, ah-lah z teng Gyi dô-kô læ keh-zo sing-jün sæn-zông cü-kwu tu tʽing-meng go.

<sup>16</sup> 阿拉已經通知爾阿拉主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>執掌權柄再來個事幹，並弗是用乖巧捏造個說話，是親眼看見其個威嚴。<sup>17</sup> 就是從箇光明個榮華裏向有介一個聲音等其話，「箇是我親愛個兒子，我頂得意個。」\[Mt. 17. 5\] 箇個時候天父神明賜撥其體面等榮華。<sup>18</sup> 箇從天來個聲音，阿拉是等其大家來箇座聖善山上主顧都聽聞個。

<sup>19</sup> Hwông-tsʽia ah-lah yiu sin-cü-nying-go shih-wô hao tso keng-kô kyʽüoh-jih-go bing-kyü: gyi kʽo-pi ih-kwun liang tin læ heh-en-go u-sen. Ng-lah kʽeng kwu-djôh gyi, dzih-tao tʽin-liang ng-kang-hyiao cʽih-kyʽi tsiao-djôh ng-lah sing go z-ʽeo, z ng-go ih-cʽü. <sup>20</sup> Dæn-z di-ih, yüong tsʽeng-ding-tông, væn-pah sin-cü-nying-go shih-wô tu m-yiu gyi-zi-go i-s læ-tih. <sup>21</sup> Sin-cü-nying-go shih-wô dzong m̆-teh cʽih-ü nying-go cü-i; tu z Jing-ming-go sing-jün nying be Sing-Ling ken-dong sô kông go.

<sup>19</sup> 況且阿拉有先知人個說話好做更加確實個憑據。其可比一管亮點來黑暗個烏碎。爾拉肯顧着其，直到天亮五更曉出去照着爾拉心個時候，是爾個益處。<sup>20</sup> 但是第一，用忖停當，凡百先知人個說話都嘸有其自個意思來的。<sup>21</sup> 先知人個說話從嘸得出於人個主意；都是神明個聖善人被聖靈感動所講個。

### 2P. II.

Tsih-z dzong-zin læ pah-sing-go cong-nyiang yiu kô sin-cü-nying; z-ka tsiang-læ ng-lah-go cong-nyiang yia pih-ding yiu kô-dao-nying. Gyi-lah we s-ʽô tsʽæn-tsing mih-vông-go zia-kyiao, zing-ts-ü feh tsiao-jing keh-we cʽü-joh gyi-lah go Cü; z-ka s-teh gyi-zi-go mih-vông soh-soh ziu tao. <sup>2</sup> Yiu hyü-to nying yia-we keng-leh gyi tso ʽô-tsôh peh-kʽæn-go z-ken, s-teh nying-kô we-leh gyi pông-hwe tsing dao-li. <sup>3</sup> Gyi-lah dzeng tʽen-sing, kông hong-pʽin shih-wô pô ng-lah tông sang-i-kying tso. Gyi-lah tsao-yi ding-kæn-go ying-vah feh-we tæn-koh; gyi-lah mih-vông-go nyih-ts yia feh-we kwʽeng-joh.

只是從前來百姓個中央有假先知人；是介將來爾拉個中央也必定有假道人。其拉會私下摻進滅亡個邪教，甚至於弗招認箇位取贖其拉個主；是介使得其自個滅亡速速就到。<sup>2</sup> 有許多人也會跟勒其做下作不堪個事幹，使得人家爲了其謗毀真道理。<sup>3</sup> 其拉存貪心，講哄騙說話把爾拉當生意經做。其拉早已定間個刑罰弗會耽擱；其拉滅亡個日子也弗會睏熟。

<sup>4</sup> Jing-ming kyi-jün feh kwʽun-shü keh-sing væn-ze-go tʽin-s, ziu vah gyi lôh di-nyüoh, pô heh-en tông lin-diao kwun-siu gyi tao sing-pʽun-go nyih-ts; <sup>5</sup> yi feh kwʽun-shü zông-kwu-ts-shü, kông-lôh ʽong-shü djü-mih keh hyüong-ôh-go shü-dæ, tsih liu-cʽih djün dao-yi go Nô-üô keh-kô pah-go nying; <sup>6</sup> yi hwe-diao Sô-to-mô Ngo-mo-lah-go dzing-ts, djong-djong ying-vah gyi, hao tso pông-yiang kying-kyiæ tsiang-læ-go ôh-nying; <sup>7</sup> tsih kyiu-cʽih kong-yi-go Lo-teh, ziu-z z-djông iu-meng keh-sing ôh-nying ʽô-tsôh ʽang-we go cü-kwu. <sup>8</sup> Keh-go kong-yi nying wa deng-læ gyi-lah cong-nyiang, nyih-nyih kʽen-kyin tʽing-meng gyi-lah wang-ying peh-fah go ʽang-we, gyi kong-yi-go sing feh-cü dza næn-ko. <sup>9</sup> Kʽo-kyin Cü z hyiao-teh dza-go hao pao-wu gyin-dzing cü-kwu tʽeh-cʽih mi-ʽôh, dza-go hao kwun-siu feh-kong-yi cü-kwu teng-tao sing-pʽun nyih-ts ziu ying-vah.

<sup>4</sup> 神明既然弗寬恕箇星犯罪個天使，就罰其落地獄，把黑暗當鏈條管守其到審判個日子；<sup>5</sup> 又弗寬恕上古之世，降落洪水除滅箇兇惡個世代，只留出傳道義個<u>挪亞</u>箇家八個人；<sup>6</sup> 又毀掉<u>所多瑪</u>、<u>蛾摩拉</u>個城子，重重刑罰其，好做榜樣警戒將來個惡人；<sup>7</sup> 只救出公義個羅得，就是時常憂悶箇星惡人下作行爲個主顧。<sup>8</sup> 箇個公義人還庉來其拉中央，日日看見聽聞其拉橫行不法個行爲，其公義個心弗知咋難過。<sup>9</sup> 可見主是曉得咋個好保護虔誠主顧脫出迷惑，咋個好管守弗公義主顧等到審判日子受刑罰。

<sup>10</sup> Hwông-tsʽia keh-sing i-dzong nyüoh-sing ao-tsao-go s-yüôh, miao-z wông-fah go cü-kwu ma? Gyi-lah z yüong-gyiang, ʽeo-zi, lin pông-hwe kwun-tsiang tu feh-pʽô. <sup>11</sup> Ziu-z tʽin-s, lih-liang neng-ken pi gyi keng-kô do, yia feh-ken læ Cü-go min-zin kông pông-hwe-go shih-wô kao gyi. <sup>12</sup> Dæn-z keh-sing nying, tsing-ziang sang-dzing m-li-sing-go yia-siu, deh-we sang-cʽih-læ peh nying kʽô-læ sah-diao go, læ-tih pông-hwe zi sô feh hyiao-teh go z-ken; ziu iao teh-djôh ôh-nyih-go pao-ing; læ zi-go doh-sing li-hyiang mih-diao; <sup>13</sup> gyi-lah tsʽing-do-bah-nyih hwun-hyi kyʽüoh-tsiu tsôh-lôh. Teng ng-lah dong-zih go z-ʽeo, z læ-tih ziah zi kæn-tsô-go mi-dao, jih-dzæ z-go tsih-le, z-go pæn-pô. <sup>14</sup> Gyi-lah mun-ngæn tu z <sup>\[1\]</sup>kæn-ying; væn-ze z ih-ngæn hyih-feh-læ; djông-djông hong-pʽin sing feh-kyin go cü-kwu, lôh gyi kyʽün-tʽao. Tsô dong-din go fông-fah, gyi-lah yi-kying tang-mo-joh; jih-dzæ z-go ih-pæn kʽo-u-go cong-tông. <sup>15</sup> Gyi-lah li-kʽæ dzih-lu, w̆æn-cün tsʽô-lu, keng-leh Pʽo-shoh-go ng-ts Pô-læn ka tseo; <sup>16</sup> keh Pô-læn tʽen fi-yi-ts-dzæ, ziu we-leh gyi feh ʽeh-li go z-ken be tsah-vah; yiu ih-tsah ô-kʽeo-go cong-sang we kông nying-go shih-wô kying-djü keh sin-cü-nying-go gwông-vông.

<sup>10</sup> 況且箇星依從肉身鏖糟個私慾，藐視王法個主顧嗎？其拉是用強，候自，連謗毀官長都弗怕。<sup>11</sup> 就是天使，力量能幹比其更加大，也弗敢來主個面前講謗毀個說話告其。<sup>12</sup> 但是箇星人，正像生成嘸理性個野獸，特爲生出來撥人抲來殺掉個，來的謗毀自所弗曉得個事幹；就要得着惡業個報應；來自個毒心裏向滅掉。<sup>13</sup> 其拉清大白日歡喜喫酒作樂。等爾拉同席個時候，是來的嚼自奸詐個味道，實在是個漬洡、是個瘢疤。<sup>14</sup> 其拉滿眼都是姦淫<sup>\[1\]</sup>；犯罪是一眼歇弗來；常常哄騙心弗堅個主顧，落其圈套。詐銅鈿個方法，其拉已經打磨熟；實在是個一班可惡個種黨。<sup>15</sup> 其拉離開直路，彎轉岔路，跟勒<u>破朔</u>個兒子<u>巴蘭</u>介走；<sup>16</sup> 箇<u>巴蘭</u>貪非義之財，就爲了其弗合理個事幹被責罰；有一隻啞口個衆生會講人個說話禁住箇先知人個狂妄。

<sup>\[1\] *ʽÔh* *wô*, ying-vu. 或話，淫婦。（原註）</sup>

<sup>17</sup> Gyi-lah z m-shü-go tsing; gwông-fong kyih-sæn-go yüing; yiu moh-tsʽeh-di-en-go u-sen teng-gyi-kæn, üong-yün peh gyi deng. <sup>18</sup> Gyi-lah kông cʽih-kah-go do-wô, tseo nyüoh-sing s-yüôh-go cʽü, ziu ying-yiu keh-sing kông-kông tʽeh-cʽih zia-lu go cü-kwu, tih-lôh gyi kyʽün-tʽao. <sup>19</sup> Gyi-lah ing-hyü nying-kô tso sih-fông-go nying, zi fæn-cün tso ze-ôh-go nu-boh; ing-we ih-go nying be jü sô cü-voh, ziu-z jü-go nu-boh.

<sup>17</sup> 其拉是嘸水個井；狂風擊散個雲；有墨漆地暗個烏碎等其間，永遠撥其庉。<sup>18</sup> 其拉講出格個大話，走肉身私慾個處，就引誘箇星剛剛脫出邪路個主顧，跌落其圈套。<sup>19</sup> 其拉應許人家做釋放個人，自反轉做罪惡個奴僕；因爲一個人被誰所制服，就是誰個奴僕。

<sup>20</sup> Ziah-yiu nying sih-teh Kyiu-Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh yi-kying tʽeh-cʽih shü-dzoh-go w̆e-u, ʽeo-deo tsæ be gyi sô dzin-ziao, sô cü-voh, gyi-lah kwe-keng pi zin-deo keng-kô li-ʽæ. <sup>21</sup> Ing-we sô djün-ziu gyi, keh sing-jün-go dao-li, ziah-z sih-teh ts-ʽeo we-be gyi, wa feh jü feh sih-teh keh-da dao-yi-go lu. <sup>22</sup> Ziu ziang <sup>\[1\]</sup>dzoh-yin wô, “Wun-kyi cün-kyʽi kyʽüoh zi tʽu-cʽih-go tong-si”; wa-yiu, “Gyiang ken-zing go nyi-cü yi kyʽi nyin-læ nyi-den li,”—— tsing-tsing z ing-djôh keh-sing nying.

<sup>20</sup> 若有人識得救主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>已經脫出世俗個穢污，後頭再被其所纏擾、所制服，其拉歸根比前頭更加厲害。<sup>21</sup> 因爲所傳授其，箇聖善個道理，若是識得之後違背其，還弗如弗識得箇埭道義個路。<sup>22</sup> 就像俗言<sup>\[1\]</sup>話，「黃狗轉去喫自吐出個東西；」還有，「滰乾淨個泥豬又去碾來泥潭裏，」——真正是應着箇星人。

<sup>\[1\] *ʽÔh* *wô*, tseng-yin. 或話，箴言。（原註）</sup>

### 2P. III.

Tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di, ngô næn-kæn sia keh di-nyi fong sing peh ng-lah, iao di-sing ng, kyih-dong ng tsʽing-kyih-go sing; <sup>2</sup> s-teh ng hao tsʽeng-tao keh-sing sing-jün sin-cü-nying yü-sin kông-go shih-wô, teng ah-lah tso Kyiu-Cü-go s-du keh-go dao-li. <sup>3</sup> Di-ih iao-kying, yüong tsʽeng-leh ding-tông, kyih-sah-go z-shü pih-ding yiu kyʽing-cü-boh-zih i zi-go s-yüôh ka tso go cü-kwu, iao wô, <sup>4</sup> Gyi tsæ læ go keh-sing ing-hyü z ʽah-li ni? dzong tsu-tsong kwʽeng-joh ts-ʽeo, væn-veh dzing-gyiu ziang tʽin-di kʽæ-bih-go z-tsin ih-yiang-go. <sup>5</sup> Gyi-lah deh-we feh-kyʽi tsʽeng tông-tsʽu-ts, tsiao Jing-ming-go feng fu, ziu yiu tʽin teng di, *yiu-sing* z lu-cʽih shü-go min-teng, *yiu-sing* z dzæ-ü shü-go cong-nyiang: <sup>6</sup> *we-leh* *ka*, keh z-ʽeo-go shü-ka be shü meh-zông mih-diao. <sup>7</sup> Ziah-z yin-dzæ-go tʽin-di, yia tsiao Gyi-go feng-fu liu-lôh tong, teng-tao sing-pʽun ôh-nying mih-vông go nyih-ts, peh ho me-diao.

親愛個兄弟，我難間寫箇第二封信撥爾拉，要提醒爾，激動爾清潔個心；<sup>2</sup> 使得爾好忖到箇星聖善先知人預先講個說話，等阿拉做救主個使徒箇個道理。<sup>3</sup> 第一要緊，用忖勒停當，結煞個時世必定有輕嘴薄舌依自個私慾介做個主顧，要話，<sup>4</sup> 其再來個箇星應許是何裏呢？從祖宗睏熟之後，萬物仍舊像天地開闢個時節一樣個。<sup>5</sup> 其拉特爲弗去忖當初子，照神明個吩咐，就有天等地，有星是露出水個面頂，有星是在於水個中央。<sup>6</sup> 爲了介，箇時候個世界被水沒上滅掉。<sup>7</sup> 若是現在個天地，也照其個吩咐留落東，等到審判惡人滅亡個日子，撥火𤈦掉。

<sup>8</sup> Tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di, keh-yiang z-tʽi ng-lah feh-kʽo feh-hyiao-teh; dziu Cü kʽen-læ, ih-nyih tsing-ziang ih-tsʽin nyin, ih-tsʽin nyin tsing-ziang ih-nyih. <sup>9</sup> Cü-go ing-hyü tsæ-ve dzi-wun, ziang yiu-sing nying sön Gyi z dzi-wun ka; Gyi jih-dzæ z kwʽun-shü ah-lah, feh-iao yiu nying mih-vông,” z iao long-tsong tu we-sing-cün-i.

<sup>8</sup> 親愛個兄弟，箇樣事體爾拉弗可弗曉得。就主看來，一日正像一千年，一千年正像一日。<sup>9</sup> 主個應許再𣍐遲緩，像有星人算其是遲緩介。其實在是寬恕阿拉，弗要有人滅亡，是要攏總都回心轉意。

<sup>10</sup> Cü-go nyih-ts pih-ding ziang yia-li-go zeh ka læ; keh-nyih tʽin yiu do-do sing-hyiang we ko-kyʽi: ng-ying be mang-ho we yiang-diao; di teng di-zông-go tong-si tu we me-diao. <sup>11</sup> Keh-sing tong-si kyi-jün tu iao hwô-diao, ng-lah tso-nying tông-kæ dza-nyiang tsʽing-kyih, dza-nyiang gyin-dzing; <sup>12</sup> hyüih-sing læ-tih <sup>\[1\]</sup>siang-vông kyʽi-mo Jing-ming-go nyih-ts hyin-cʽih-læ, ziu-z tʽin be ho hwô-diao, ng-ying be ho yiang-diao keh-nyih. <sup>13</sup> Ah-lah tsiao Gyi-go ing-hyü z siang-vông tsʽoh-sing-go tʽin, tsʽoh-sing-go di, dao-yi sô deng-go di-fông. <sup>14</sup> Sô-yi, tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di, kyi-jün siang-vông keh-sing z-ken, yüong zông-kying tso m yüô-tin m pʽi-bing go nying, hao en-en-sing-sing kyʽi kyin Cü. <sup>15</sup> Yia kæ tsʽeng-tao ah-lah Cü-go kwʽun-shü z we-leh kyiu-go i-s; ziang ah-lah tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di Pao-lo, tsiao sô s-peh gyi go tsʽong-ming yi-kying sia sing peh ng-lah. <sup>16</sup> Gyi long-tsong-go shü-sing li tu yiu-kông keh-sing z-ken; nen-cong yiu-sing shih-wô næn-yi ming-bah; keh-sing m̆-teh ʽôh-we, sing feh-kyin go cü-kwu min-kyʽiang ka-shih gyi; yia ziang yü-to-go Sing-shü ka; z long-cʽih zi-go ʽo-se læ. <sup>17</sup> Keh-lah, tsʽing-æ-go hyüong-di, ng-lah kyi-jün yü-sin hyiao-teh, yüong tso-gyi, sæn-leh be ôh-nying-go tsʽoh-ngwu sô ying-yiu, shih-diao zi-go pô-oh. <sup>18</sup> Wa-yiu yüong kô-tsʽeo ah-lah Kyiu-Cü Yiæ-su Kyi-toh-go eng-we teng cü-sih. Dæn-nyün yüong-wô dzong yin-dzæ ih-dzih tao m gyüong-dzing go shü-dæ tu kwe-peh Gyi. Üô-meng.

<sup>10</sup> 主個日子必定像夜裏個賊介來。箇日天有大大聲響會過去；五行被猛火會煬掉；地等地上個東西都會𤈦掉。<sup>11</sup> 箇星東西既然都要化掉，爾拉做人當該咋樣清潔，咋樣虔誠；<sup>12</sup> 血心來的想望<sup>\[1\]</sup>企慕神明個日子顯出來，就是天被火化掉，五行被火煬掉箇日。<sup>13</sup> 阿拉照其個應許是想望簇新個天，簇新個地，道義所庉個地方。<sup>14</sup> 所以，親愛個兄弟，既然想望箇星事幹，用上緊做嘸瑕點、嘸批評個人，好安安心心去見主。<sup>15</sup> 也該忖到阿拉主個寬恕是爲了救個意思；像阿拉親愛個兄弟<u>保羅</u>，照所賜撥其個聰明已經寫信撥爾拉。<sup>16</sup> 其攏總個書信裏都有講箇星事幹；內中有星說話難以明白；箇星嘸得學會、心弗堅個主顧勉強解說其；也像餘多個聖書介；是弄出自個禍祟來。<sup>17</sup> 箇拉，親愛個兄弟，爾拉既然預先曉得，用做忌，省勒被惡人個錯誤所引誘，失掉自個把握。<sup>18</sup> 還有用加湊阿拉救主<u>耶穌</u> <u>基督</u>個恩惠等知識。但願榮華從現在一直到嘸窮盡個世代都歸撥其。亞們。
