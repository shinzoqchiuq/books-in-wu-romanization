## CʽIH YIÆ-GYIH KYI. 出<u>埃及</u>记

### C. I.

YI-SEH-LIH keh-sing ng-ts teng Yüô-koh tao Yiæ-gyih, koh-nying teng gyi kô-kyün dô-kô læ-go, gyi ming-z læ ʽô-veng. <sup>2</sup> Liu-bin, Si-min, Li-vi, Yiu-da, <sup>3</sup> Yi-sah-kyüô, Si-pu-leng, Bin-yüô-ming, <sup>4</sup> Dæn, Neh-da-li, Kyüô-teh, Üô-shih. <sup>5</sup> Væn-pah dzong Yüô-koh iao-bo-li sang-loh-læ-go, tʽong-gong tsʽih-jih-go nying. Iah-seh z yi-kying læ Yiæ-gyih de. <sup>6</sup> Iah-seh teng cong hyüong-di, lin tso-dæ-go nying, tu si-de. <sup>7</sup> Yi-seh-lih ts-seng sang-yiang, meo-meo zing-zing to-kyʽi-læ, do-nyiang hying-wông, cʽong-mun keh-go di-fông.

<u>以色列</u>箇星儿子等<u>雅各</u>到<u>埃及</u>，各人等其家眷大家来个，其名字来下文。<sup>2</sup> <u>流便</u>、<u>西缅</u>、<u>利未</u>、<u>犹大</u>、<sup>3</sup> <u>以萨迦</u>、<u>西布伦</u>、<u>便雅悯</u>、<sup>4</sup> <u>但</u>、<u>纳大利</u>、<u>迦得</u>、<u>亚设</u>。<sup>5</sup> 凡百从<u>雅各</u>腰缚里生落来个，统共七十个人。<u>约瑟</u>是已经来<u>埃及</u>了。<sup>6</sup> <u>约瑟</u>等众兄弟、连做代个人，都死了。<sup>7</sup> <u>以色列</u>子孙生养，茂茂盛盛多起来，大样兴旺，充满箇个地方。

<sup>8</sup> Ka z-ʽeo Yiæ-gyih koh yiu ih-go sing wông tsih-we, feh hyiao-teh Iah-seh-go. <sup>9</sup> Teng gyi pah-sing wô, Ng kʽen, Yi-seh-lih ts-seng keh-dzoh to-jü ah-lah, yi gyiang-jü ah-lah. <sup>10</sup> Læ, ah-lah hao yüong kyi-meo dæ gyi, sæn-leh tsæ to-kyʽi-læ; ʽeo-deo pʽong-djoh tang-tsiang z-ʽeo, gyi-lah ʽeh-leh dziu-dih kong-kyih ah-lah, li-kʽæ tsʽ-di cʽih-kyʽi. <sup>11</sup> Ziu shih-lih toh-kong go kyʽi kwun gyi-lah, pih gyi tʽiao-tæn. Gyi-lah we-leh Fah-lao kyʽi-zao Pi-tong, Læn-seh, liang-go dzing-ts tso kwʽu-vông. <sup>12</sup> Dæn-z yüih pih-næn gyi, yüih-we kô-tsʽeo, di-fông yüih-fah tsin-kʽæ-læ. Z-ka gyi-lah we-leh Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tæn sing-z. <sup>13</sup> Yiæ-gyih nying ngang-kʽô Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tso djong-deo sang-weh, <sup>14</sup> peh gyi sing-sing kwʽu-kwʽu ko nyih-ts, sông zah-hwe, siao cün-deo, teng pah-yiang din-li-go kong-dzing; væn-pah sô yüong-kong-go z-ken tu kwun-leh nyin.

<sup>8</sup> 介时候<u>埃及</u>国有一个新王即位，弗晓得<u>约瑟</u>个。<sup>9</sup> 等其百姓话，「尔看，<u>以色列</u>子孙箇族多如阿拉，又强如阿拉。<sup>10</sup> 来，阿拉好用计谋待其，省勒再多起来；后头碰着打账时候，其拉合勒仇敌攻击阿拉，离开此地出去。」<sup>11</sup> 就设立督工个去管其拉，逼其挑担。其拉为了<u>法老</u>起造<u>比东</u>、<u>兰塞</u>，两个城子做库房。<sup>12</sup> 但是越逼难其，越会加凑，地方越发展开来。是介其拉为了<u>以色列</u>子孙担心事。<sup>13</sup> <u>埃及</u>人硬抲<u>以色列</u>子孙做重头生活，<sup>14</sup> 拨其辛辛苦苦过日子，舂石灰、烧砖头、等百样田里个工程。凡百所用工个事干都管勒严。

<sup>15</sup> Hyi-pah-læ siu-sang go nyü-nying, ih-go ming-z Jih-lah, ih-go ming-z Bu-üô: Yiæ-gyih wông feng-fu gyi-lah, <sup>16</sup> wô, Ng-lah teng Hyi-pah-læ nyü-nying siu-sang, kʽen gyi zông zo-dong z-ʽeo, ziah-z w̆æn, iao long gyi sah; ziah-z nyiang-ts hao peh gyi weh. <sup>17</sup> Dæn-z siu-sang-go pʽô-gyü Jing-ming, feh tsiao Yiæ-gyih wông go feng-fu ka tso, pao-djün nen-w̆æn sing-ming. <sup>18</sup> Yiæ-gyih wông eo siu-sang-go, teng gyi-lah wô, Ng dza-we ka tso, pao-djün nen-w̆æn sing-ming ni? <sup>19</sup> Siu-sang-go teng Fah-lao wô, Hyi-pah-læ nyü-nying teng Yiæ-gyih nyü-nying koh-yiang: gyi-lah z-ka gyin; siu-sang-go feh-zing tao, ng-ts yi-kying sang-loh-de. <sup>20</sup> We-leh ka, Jing-ming s eng peh siu-sang-go: ping-tsʽia pah-sing to-kyʽi-læ, keng-kô gyiang-zing. <sup>21</sup> Ing-we siu-sang-go pʽô-gyü Jing-ming, keh-lah s-teh gyi-lah dzing-kô-lih-nyih. <sup>22</sup> Fah-lao feng-fu gyi cong pah-sing, wô, Gyi-lah sô sang-go nen-w̆æn, ng-lah pih-iao tiu-læ ʽo-li, tsih-yiu keh-sing nyiang-ts, hao pao-djün gyi sing-ming.

<sup>15</sup> <u>希伯来</u>收生个女人，一个名字<u>实拉</u>，一个名字<u>部亚</u>。<u>埃及</u>王吩咐其拉，<sup>16</sup> 话，「尔拉等<u>希伯来</u>女人收生，看其上坐桶时候，若是娃，要弄其煞；若是娘子好拨其活。」<sup>17</sup> 但是收生个怕惧神明，弗照<u>埃及</u>王个吩咐介做，保全男娃性命。<sup>18</sup> <u>埃及</u>王讴收生个，等其拉话，「尔咋会介做，保全男娃性命呢？」<sup>19</sup> 收生个等<u>法老</u>话，「<u>希伯来</u>女人等<u>埃及</u>女人各样；其拉是介健；收生个弗曾到，儿子已经生落了。」<sup>20</sup> 为了介，神明赐恩拨收生个。并且百姓多起来，更加强盛。<sup>21</sup> 因为收生个怕惧神明，箇拉使得其拉成家立业。<sup>22</sup> <u>法老</u>吩咐其众百姓，话，「其拉所生个男娃，尔拉必要丢来河里；只有箇星娘子，好保全其性命。」

### C. II.

KA z-ʽeo Li-vi keh-dzoh yiu ih-go nying kyʽi dæ Li-vi nying-go nön. <sup>2</sup> Keh nyü-nying yiu sing-yüing, sang ng-ts: kʽen gyi hao-kʽen, kʽông-leh sæn-ko yüih. <sup>3</sup> ʽEo-deo tsæ kʽông-feh-djü, teng gyi do ih-tsah lu-ken tso-go siang-ts, yüong zah-yiu teng jü-tsiang du-leh gyi; li-hyiang fông-leh na-hwun; en-læ ʽo-pin-yin lu-za-vong-li. <sup>4</sup> Gyi ah-tsi yün-yün lih-tong kʽen gyi yiu soh-go pʽong-djoh. <sup>5</sup> Kʽeo-kʽeo Fah-lao-go nön tseo-tao ʽo-pin-yin kyʽi gyiang-nyüoh: gyi-go s-nyü læ ʽo-pin-yin tseo-læ tseo-kyʽi: gyi-zi kʽen-kyin siang-ts læ lu-za-vong-li, ziu tsʽa s-nyü kyʽi do-læ. <sup>6</sup> Kʽæ-kʽæ-læ kʽen-kyin na-hwun: na-hwun læ-tih kyiao. Ziu æ-lin gyi, wô, Keh z Hyi-pah-læ nying-go ng-ts. <sup>7</sup> Keh-tsao gyi ah-tsi teng Fah-lao-go nön wô, Ngô kyʽi eo Hyi-pah-læ ah-bu, teng ng iang keh-go na-hwun, hao s-teh feh? <sup>8</sup> Fah-lao-go nön teng gyi wô, Ng kyʽi. Keh nyiang-ts ziu kyʽi, eo na-hwun-go ah-nyiang læ. <sup>9</sup> Fah-lao-go nön teng gyi wô, Bao keh-go na-hwun kyʽi, teng ngô iang gyi; ngô we coh kong-din peh ng. Keh nyü-nying ziu bao na-hwun kyʽi iang gyi. <sup>10</sup> Na-hwun do-kyʽi-læ; nyü-nying ling gyi tao Fah-lao nön-go di-fông, ziu tso gyi-go ng-ts. Fah-lao nön cʽü gyi ming-z <sup>\[1\]</sup>Mo-si; ing-we gyi wô, Ngô dzong shü-li l̆a gyi zông-læ-go.

介时候<u>利未</u>箇族有一个人去抬<u>利未</u>人个囡。<sup>2</sup> 箇女人有身孕，生儿子。看其好看，囥勒三个月。<sup>3</sup> 后头再囥弗住，等其驮一只芦竿做个箱子，用石油等乳浆涂勒其；里向放勒奶花；安来河边沿芦柴缝里。<sup>4</sup> 其阿姊远远立东看其有啥个碰着。<sup>5</sup> 扣扣<u>法老</u>个囡走到河边沿去滰肉。其个使女来河边沿走来走去。其自看见箱子来芦柴缝里，就差使女去驮来。<sup>6</sup> 开开来看见奶花。奶花来的叫。就哀怜其，话，「箇是<u>希伯来</u>人个儿子。」<sup>7</sup> 箇遭其阿姊等<u>法老</u>个囡话，「我去讴<u>希伯来</u>阿哺，等尔养箇个奶花，好使得弗？」<sup>8</sup> <u>法老</u>个囡等其话，「尔去。」箇娘子就去，讴奶花个阿娘来。<sup>9</sup> <u>法老</u>个囡等其话，「抱箇个奶花去，等我养其；我会给工钿拨尔。」箇女人就抱奶花去养其。<sup>10</sup> 奶花大起来。女人领其到<u>法老</u>囡个地方，就做其个儿子。<u>法老</u>囡取其名字<u>摩西</u><sup>\[1\]</sup>。因为其话，「我从水里拉其上来个。」

<sup>\[1\] *Ziu-z* Te-cʽih. 就是𠡒出。（原注）</sup>

<sup>11</sup> Mo-si công-nyin z-ʽeo, tseo-cʽih tao gyi hyüong-di di-fông, kʽen gyi-lah tʽiao-tæn; kʽeo-kʽeo kʽen-kyin Yiæ-gyih nying tang ih-go Hyi-pah-læ nying, ziu-z gyi-go hyüong-di. <sup>12</sup> Kʽen-kʽen dông-pin keh-pin, tu m-nying læ-tong, ziu tang-sah keh Yiæ-gyih nying, kʽông gyi læ sô-nyi-li. <sup>13</sup> Di-nyi nyih, yi tseo-cʽih-kyʽi; kʽen-kyin liang-go Hyi-pah-læ nying siang-tang. Mo-si teng feh-dzing-li go nying wô, Ng dza-we tang ng zi-kô-nying ni? <sup>14</sup> Gyi wô, Jü lih ng tso moh-pah tso kwun, hao pʽun-tön ah-lah z-ken ma? Ng siang long-sah ngô, ziang long-sah Yiæ-gyih nying ka ma? Mo-si kyʽih-hoh, wô, Keh-go en-gyin ko-jün pʽo-de. <sup>15</sup> Fah-lao tʽing-meng keh-kyʽi z-ken ziu iao sah Mo-si. Dæn-z Mo-si bi-ko Fah-lao min-zin, tao Mi-din di-fông kyʽi; zo-læ tsing pin-yin. <sup>16</sup> Mi-din-go tsi-s yiu tsʽih-go nön; kʽeo-kʽeo læ tang shü, tsi-mun keh-sing shü-zao, hao s gyi ah-tia-go yiang-de kyʽüoh shü. <sup>17</sup> Keh-sing kʽen-yiang-go læ, ken gyi-lah tseo; Mo-si ziu lih-kyʽi-læ, pông-dzu gyi-lah, peh gyi yiang-de kyʽüoh shü. <sup>18</sup> Gyi-lah ih kyü-tao ah-tia Liu-r di-fông, gyi wô, Kyih-mih-ts, ng dza-we kyü-læ ka kwʽa? <sup>19</sup> Gyi-lah wô, Yiu ih-go Yiæ-gyih nying kyiu ah-lah cʽih keh sing kʽen-yiang nying siu-li; yi teng ah-lah tang iao yüong-go shü, peh yiang-de kyʽüoh. <sup>20</sup> Gyi teng nön wô, Keh-go nying læ ʽah-li? ng-lah dza-we tsʽo-loh gyi? Hao tsʽing gyi læ kyʽüoh-ping. <sup>21</sup> Mo-si dzing-nyün teng keh-go nying dô-kô deng: gyi pô nön Si-pʽo-lah peh Mo-si tso tsʽi-ts. <sup>22</sup> Gyi tsʽi-ts sang ng-ts; Mo-si cʽü gyi ming-z <sup>\[1\]</sup>Keh-jing, z-ka wô, Ngô læ nga-deo di-fông tso cʽih-meng nying.

<sup>11</sup> <u>摩西</u>壮年时候，走出到其兄弟地方，看其拉挑担。扣扣看见<u>埃及</u>人打一个<u>希伯来</u>人，就是其个兄弟。<sup>12</sup> 看看荡边箇边，都呒人来东，就打煞箇<u>埃及</u>人，囥其来沙泥里。<sup>13</sup> 第二日，又走出去；看见两个<u>希伯来</u>人相打。<u>摩西</u>等弗成礼个人话，「尔咋会打尔自家人呢？」<sup>14</sup> 其话，「谁立尔做牧伯做官，好判断阿拉事干吗？尔想弄煞我，像弄煞<u>埃及</u>人介吗？」<u>摩西</u>吃𢞕，话，「箇个案件果然破了。」<sup>15</sup> <u>法老</u>听闻箇起事干就要杀<u>摩西</u>。但是<u>摩西</u>避过<u>法老</u>面前，到<u>米甸</u>地方去；坐来井边沿。<sup>16</sup> <u>米甸</u>个祭司有七个囡。扣扣来打水，𣥼满箇星水槽，好使其阿爹个羊队吃水。<sup>17</sup> 箇星看羊个来，赶其拉走。<u>摩西</u>就立起来，帮助其拉，拨其羊队吃水。<sup>18</sup> 其拉一归到阿爹<u>流珥</u>地方，其话，「今末子，尔咋会归来介快？」<sup>19</sup> 其拉话，「有一个<u>埃及</u>人救阿拉出箇星看羊人手里；又等阿拉打要用个水，拨羊队吃。」<sup>20</sup> 其等囡话，「箇个人来何里？尔拉咋会错落其？好请其来吃饼。」<sup>21</sup> <u>摩西</u>情愿等箇个人大家庉。其把囡<u>西坡拉</u>拨<u>摩西</u>做妻子。<sup>22</sup> 其妻子生儿子。<u>摩西</u>取其名字<u>革顺</u><sup>\[1\]</sup>，是介话，「我来外头地方做出门人。」

<sup>\[1\] *Ziu-z* Cʽih-meng nying. 就是出门人。（原注）</sup>

<sup>23</sup> Ko-leh dziang-kyiu Yiæ-gyih wông si-de; Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, we-leh ziu lao-kwʽu, tʽæn-kyʽi eo-hyiang; gyi lao-kwʽu-go sing-hyiang zông-kyʽi peh Jing-ming tʽing. <sup>24</sup> Jing-ming tʽing-meng gyi-lah tʽæn-kyʽi-go sing-hyiang; ziu kyi-teh zin-deo teng Üô-pah-lah-hen, Yi-sah, Yüô-koh, shih-lih-go iah. <sup>25</sup> Jing-ming kʽen-kwu Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, tsiao-kwun gyi-lah.

<sup>23</sup> 过了长久<u>埃及</u>王死了。<u>以色列</u>子孙，为了受劳苦，叹气讴响；其劳苦个声响上去拨神明听。<sup>24</sup> 神明听闻其拉叹气个声响；就记得前头等<u>亚伯拉罕</u>、<u>以撒</u>、<u>雅各</u>设立个约。<sup>25</sup> 神明看顾<u>以色列</u>子孙，照管其拉。

### C. III.

KEH-TSAO Mo-si kʽen gyi dziang-nying Mi-din tsi-s Yih-lo-go yiang, ling gyi tao kwʽông-iæ nga-pin, tseo-tao Jing-ming-go sæn, ziu-z ʽO-lih sæn. <sup>2</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go Tʽin-s dzong tsʽ-bang-jü ho-yin cong-nyiang hyin-cʽih-læ peh Mo-si kʽen. Gyi ih-kʽen, kʽen-kyin tsʽ-bang-jü ho læ-tih dziah, tsʽ-bang m-neh me-diao. <sup>3</sup> Mo-si wô, Næn-kæn ngô tao-tseo-cün kʽen keh-go do gyi-z, tsʽ-bang feh me-diao z dza-go yün-kwu. <sup>4</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô kʽen-kyin gyi tseo-cün læ kʽen, Jing-ming dzong tsʽ-bang-li eo gyi, wô, Mo-si, Mo-si! Gyi wô, Ngô læ-tong. <sup>5</sup> Jing-ming yi wô, M-nao tseo-long-læ: tʽeh-diao ng kyiah-li-go ʽa, ing-we ng lih-tih-go di-fông z sing-jün di-fông. <sup>6</sup> Yi wô, Ngô z ng ah-tia-go Jing-ming, Üô-pah-lah-hen-go Jing-ming, Yi-sah-go Jing-ming, Yüô-koh-go Jing-ming. Mo-si tsô-leh min-kʽong, ing-we pʽô kʽen-kyin Jing-ming yün-kwu. <sup>7</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô wô, Ngô yi-kying kʽen-kyin Ngô-go pah-sing læ Yiæ-gyih-go kwʽu-tsʽu, yi tʽing-meng gyi-lah we-leh; toh-kong go, wæ-wæ eo-hyiang: Ngô hyiao-teh gyi-lah-go iu-meng. <sup>8</sup> Ngô kông-loh-læ kyiu gyi-lah tʽeh-cʽih Yiæ-gyih nying-go siu-li, ling gyi-lah cʽih keh-go di-ka, tao kwʽun-kwʽeh, ʽeo-jih, liu na-na teng mih-dông-go di-tʽu; ziu-z Kyüô-nen, Heh, Üô-mo-li, Pi-li-si, Hyi-vi, Yiæ-pu-z, keh-sing dzoh-veng-go di-fông. <sup>9</sup> Næn-kæn Ngô tʽing-meng Yi-seh-lih ts-seng hyiang-hyiang gyiu-kʽeng Ngô; Ngô yia kʽen-kyin Yiæ-gyih nying mo-næn gyi-go kwʽu-tsʽu. <sup>10</sup> Keh-lah ng læ; Ngô we tsʽa ng kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, ling Ngô-go pah-sing Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tseo-cʽih Yiæ-gyih.

箇遭<u>摩西</u>看其丈人<u>米甸</u>祭司<u>叶罗</u>个羊，领其到旷野外边，走到神明个山，就是<u>何烈</u>山。<sup>2</sup> 耶和华个天使从刺棚树火焰中央显出来拨<u>摩西</u>看。其一看，看见刺棚树火来的着，刺棚呒得𤈦掉。<sup>3</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「难间我倒走转看箇个大奇事，刺棚弗𤈦掉是咋个缘故。」<sup>4</sup> 耶和华看见其走转来看，神明从刺棚里讴其，话，「<u>摩西</u>，<u>摩西</u>！」其话，「我来东。」<sup>5</sup> 神明又话，「呒得好走拢来。脱掉尔脚里个鞋，因为尔立的个地方是圣善地方。」<sup>6</sup> 又话，「我是尔阿爹个神明，<u>亚伯拉罕</u>个神明，<u>以撒</u>个神明，<u>雅各</u>个神明。」<u>摩西</u>遮勒面孔，因为怕看见神明缘故。<sup>7</sup> 耶和华话，「我已经看见我个百姓来<u>埃及</u>个苦楚，又听闻其拉为了督工个怀怀讴响。我晓得其拉个忧闷。<sup>8</sup> 我降落来救其拉脱出<u>埃及</u>人个手里，领其拉出箇个地界，到宽阔、厚实、流奶奶等蜜糖个地土。就是<u>迦南</u>、<u>赫</u>、<u>亚摩利</u>、<u>比利洗</u>、<u>希未</u>、<u>耶布士</u>，箇星族份个地方。<sup>9</sup> 难间我听闻<u>以色列</u>子孙响响求恳我；我也看见<u>埃及</u>人磨难其个苦楚。<sup>10</sup> 箇拉尔来。我会差尔去见<u>法老</u>，领我个百姓<u>以色列</u>子孙走出<u>埃及</u>。」

<sup>11</sup> Mo-si teng Jing-ming wô, Ngô z soh-go nying hao kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, ling Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tseo-cʽih Yiæ-gyih ni? <sup>12</sup> Gyi wô, Ngô pih-ding we teng ng dong-de læ-tong: ping-tsʽia keh hao tso Ngô tsʽa ng-go bing-kyü: ng ling pah-sing tseo-cʽih Yiæ-gyih-go ʽeo-deo, læ keh-go sæn ng-lah we voh-z Jing-ming. <sup>13</sup> Mo-si teng Jing-ming wô, Ngô tao Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go di-fông, teng gyi-lah wô, Ng tsu-tsong-go Jing-ming tsʽa ngô læ; jü-ko gyi-lah meng ngô wô, Gyi z soh-go ming-deo? ka-ni ngô dza we-teh gyi-lah? <sup>14</sup> Jing-ming teng Mo-si wô, NGÔ Z-JÜN-R-JÜN YIU-GO: yi wô, Ng teng Yi-seh-lih ts-seng hao wô, Z-JÜN YIU-GO tsʽa ngô tao ng di-fông læ. <sup>15</sup> Jing-ming yi teng Mo-si wô, Ng teng Yi-seh-lih ts-seng hao wô, Tsʽa ngô læ-go z <sup>\[1\]</sup>Yiæ-ʽo-wô, ng-lah tsu-tsong-go Jing-ming, Üô-pah-lah-hen-go Jing-ming, Yi-sah-go Jing-ming, Yüô-koh-go Jing-ming; keh z Ngô üong-yün-go ming-deo! keh z Ngô shü-shü dæ-dæ-go kyi-ʽao. <sup>16</sup> Ng kyʽi jü-long Yi-seh-lih-go tsiang-lao, teng gyi-lah wô, Ng tsu-tsong-go Jing-ming, Üô-pah-lah-hen-go Jing-ming, Yi-sah-go Jing-ming, Yüô-koh-go Jing-ming, Yiæ-ʽo-wô hyin-cʽih peh ngô kʽen, wô, Ngô tsing-tsing tsiao-kwun ng-lah, ping-tsʽia Yiæ-gyih nying sô tao ng-lah-go z-ken, Ngô yi-kying kʽen-kyin-de. <sup>17</sup> Ngô yi-kying wô-ko-liao, Ngô! we ling ng-lah tʽeh-cʽih Yiæ-gyih-go kwʽu-næn, tao Kyüô-nen, Heh, Üô-mo-li, Pi-li-si, Hyi-vi, Yiæ-pu-z, keh-sing dzoh-veng-go di-fông, ziu-z liu na-na teng mih-dông-go di-fông. <sup>18</sup> Cong-nying we tʽing ng-go shih-wô: ng hao teng Yi-seh-lih tsiang-lao kyʽi kyin Yiæ-gyih wông, teng gyi wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô yi-kying we-djoh ah-lah: næn-kæn, tsʽing ng, cing ah-lah tao kwʽông-iæ di-fông sæn-nyih-go lu-dzing, kyʽi tsi Yiæ-ʽo-wô, ah-lah-go Jing-ming. <sup>19</sup> Ngô hyiao-teh ziah feh-z hyin-cʽih do neng-ken-go siu-dön, Yiæ-gyih wông feh-hyü ng-lah kyʽi. <sup>20</sup> Ngô we sing-cʽih siu læ, tso hyü-to gyi-z tang Yiæ-gyih koh: ʽeo-deo gyi kʽeng s ng kyʽi. <sup>21</sup> Ping-tsʽia Ngô we s-teh keh-go pah-sing teh-djoh eng-we læ Yiæ-gyih nying-go min-zin; ng-lah tseo-cʽih-go z-ʽeo, feh we kʽong-siu tseo-cʽih. <sup>22</sup> Ko-tang-ko nyü-nying we hyiang gyi ling-sô-kô, teng tæn-koh læ gyi oh-li-go nyü-kʽah, tʽao kying-ts nying-ts-go kyʽi-ming, teng i-zông keh-sing, peh zi-go ng-ts nön: z-ka ng-lah we lo-liah Yiæ-gyih nying-go dzæ-veh.

<sup>11</sup> <u>摩西</u>等神明话，「我是啥个人好去见<u>法老</u>，领<u>以色列</u>子孙走出<u>埃及</u>呢？」<sup>12</sup> 其话，「我必定会等尔同队来东。并且箇好做我差尔个凭据。尔领百姓走出<u>埃及</u>个后头，来箇个山尔拉会服侍神明。」<sup>13</sup> <u>摩西</u>等神明话，「我到<u>以色列</u>子孙个地方，等其拉话，尔祖宗个神明差我来。如果其拉问我话，其是啥个名头？介呢我咋回答其拉？」<sup>14</sup> 神明等<u>摩西</u>话，「我自然而然有个。」又话，「尔等<u>以色列</u>子孙好话，自然有个差我到尔地方来。」<sup>15</sup> 神明又等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔等<u>以色列</u>子孙好话，差我来个是耶和华<sup>\[1\]</sup>，尔拉祖宗个神明，<u>亚伯拉罕</u>个神明，<u>以撒</u>个神明，<u>雅各</u>个神明。箇是我永远个名头！箇是我世世代代个记号。<sup>16</sup> 尔去聚拢<u>以色列</u>个长老，等其拉话，尔祖宗个神明，<u>亚伯拉罕</u>个神明，<u>以撒</u>个神明，<u>雅各</u>个神明，耶和华显出拨我看，话，我真正照管尔拉，并且<u>埃及</u>人所到尔拉个事干，我已经看见了。<sup>17</sup> 我已经话过了，我会领尔拉脱出<u>埃及</u>个苦难，到<u>迦南</u>、<u>赫</u>、<u>亚摩利</u>、<u>比利洗</u>、<u>希未</u>、<u>耶布士</u>，箇星族份个地方，就是流奶奶等蜜糖个地方。<sup>18</sup> 众人会听尔个说话。尔好等<u>以色列</u>长老去见<u>埃及</u>王，等其话，<u>希伯来</u>人个神明耶和华已经会着阿拉。难间，请尔，准阿拉到旷野地方三日个路程，去祭耶和华，阿拉个神明。<sup>19</sup> 我晓得若弗是显出大能干个手段，<u>埃及</u>王弗许尔拉去。<sup>20</sup> 我会伸出手来，做许多奇事打<u>埃及</u>国。后头其肯赐尔去。<sup>21</sup> 并且我会使得箇个百姓得着恩惠来<u>埃及</u>人个面前。尔拉走出个时候，弗会空手走出。<sup>22</sup> 个打个女人会向其邻舍家，等耽搁来其屋里个女客，讨金子银子个器皿，等衣裳箇星，拨自个儿子囡。是介尔拉会掳掠<u>埃及</u>人个财物。」

<sup>\[1\] *Ziu-z* Z-jün-r-jün yiu-go. 就是自然而然有个。（原注）</sup>

### C. IV.

MO-SI we-teh wô, Gyi-lah feh we siang-sing ngô, feh we tʽing ngô-go shih-wô; ing-we gyi-lah we wô, Yiæ-ʽo-wô m-neh hyin-cʽih peh ng kʽen. <sup>2</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng gyi wô, Læ ng siu-li-go z soh-go tong-si? Gyi wô, Kwa-dziang. <sup>3</sup> Cü wô, Tiu gyi læ di-yiang-li: tiu-loh di-yiang, pin ih-kwang dzô; Mo-si ziu to-bi gyi. <sup>4</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Ng siu sing-cʽih, nyiah-leh gyi mi-pô: (ziu sing-cʽih siu læ, kʽô gyi; tao siu-li, dzing-gyiu z kwa-dziang: ) <sup>5</sup> s-teh gyi-lah siang-sing Yiæ-ʽo-wô, ziu-z gyi tsu-tsong-go Jing-ming, Üô-pah-lah-hen-go Jing-ming, Yi-sah-go Jing-ming, Yüô-koh-go Jing-ming, yi-kying hyin-cʽih peh ng kʽen. <sup>6</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô yi teng gyi wô, Ng siu tsʽô-tsing gwa-li: ziu pô siu tsʽô-tsing gwa-li; do-cʽih ih-kʽen, gyi siu sang da-mô-fong ziang shih ka bah. <sup>7</sup> Yi wô, Ng-go siu tsæ tsʽô-tsing gwa-li: (ziu pô siu tsæ tsʽô-tsing gwa-li: do-cʽih ih-kʽen, dzing-gyiu teng gyi yü-to-go kyi-sing ih-yiang.) <sup>8</sup> Ka-ni, ziah-z gyi-lah feh siang-sing ng, feh tʽing zin-deo bing-kyü go te-tsing, tsong we siang-sing ʽeo-deo bing-kyü go te-tsing. <sup>9</sup> Tʽông-jün gyi-lah feh siang-sing keh liang-go bing-kyü, yi feh tʽing ng-go shih-wô, ng hao do ʽo-li-go shü, tao-læ sao-di-li; ng dzong ʽo-li do-læ-go shü we læ sao-di-li pin tso hyüih. <sup>10</sup> Mo-si teng Yiæ-ʽo-wô wô, Cü ʽô, ngô ih-hyiang feh da we kông shih-wô, m-neh kʽeo-neng zih-bin; næn-kæn Ng feng-fu nu-boh ts-ʽeo, dzing-gyiu z-ka. <sup>11</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng gyi wô, Zao nying cü-pô-go z jü? Zao gyi tso ô-ts, tso long-bang, tso liang-ngæn, tso hah-ngæn-go z jü? Kyʽi feh-z Ngô Yiæ-ʽo-wô ma? <sup>12</sup> Ka-ni ng kyʽi; Ngô we pông-dzu ng cü-pô, kao ng sô ing-kæ kông-go. <sup>13</sup> Mo-si wô, Gyiu Ng, Cü, Ng sô iao tsʽa-go, hao tʽoh-fu gyi siu-li tsʽa gyi. <sup>14</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ô-wông Mo-si, ziu wô, Næn-dao m-neh Li-vi nying Üô-leng, ng-go ah-ko ma? Ngô hyiao-teh gyi we kông shih-wô: ping-tsʽia gyi tseo-cʽih-læ nying-tsih ng: kʽen-kyin ng-go z-ʽeo, gyi sing-li we hwun-hyi. <sup>15</sup> Ng hao teng gyi kông, shih-wô djün peh gyi kʽeo-li: Ngô we pông-dzu ng-go cü-pô teng gyi-go cü-pô, ping-tsʽia kao ng-lah sô tông-kæ tso-go z-ken. <sup>16</sup> Üô-leng we dæ ng wô hyiang pah-sing dao: gyi we teng ng dæ tso cü-pô, ng we teng gyi dæ? tso Jing-ming. <sup>17</sup> Ng siu-li hao nyiah keh keng kwa-dziang yüong gyi tso keh-sing gyi-z.

<u>摩西</u>回答话，「其拉弗会相信我，弗会听我个说话。因为其拉会话，耶和华呒得显出拨尔看。」<sup>2</sup> 耶和华等其话，「来尔手里个是啥个东西？」其话，「拐杖。」<sup>3</sup> 主话，「丢其来地垟里。」丢落地垟，变一梗蛇。<u>摩西</u>就躲避其。<sup>4</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔手伸出，捏勒其尾巴。」（就伸出手来，抲其；到手里，仍旧是拐杖。）<sup>5</sup> 使得其拉相信耶和华，就是其祖宗个神明、<u>亚伯拉罕</u>个神明、<u>以撒</u>个神明、<u>雅各</u>个神明，已经显出拨尔看。<sup>6</sup> 耶和华又等其话，「尔手叉进怀里。」就把手叉进怀里。驮出一看，其手生大麻风像雪介白。<sup>7</sup> 又话，「尔个手再叉进怀里。」（就把手再叉进怀里。驮出一看，仍旧等其余多个肌身一样。）<sup>8</sup> 介呢，若是其拉弗相信尔，弗听前头凭据个对证，总会相信后头凭据个对证。<sup>9</sup> 倘然其拉弗相信箇两个凭据，又弗听尔个说话，尔好驮河里个水，倒来燥地里。尔从河里驮来个水会来燥地里变做血。<sup>10</sup> <u>摩西</u>等耶和华话，「主嗄，我一向弗大会讲说话，呒得口能舌辩。难间尔吩咐奴仆之后，仍旧是介。」<sup>11</sup> 耶和华等其话，「造人嘴巴个是谁？造其做哑子、做聋𦗭、做亮眼、做瞎眼个是谁？岂弗是我耶和华吗？」<sup>12</sup> 介呢尔去。我会帮助尔嘴巴，教尔所应该讲个。<sup>13</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「求尔，主，尔所要差个，好托付其手里差其。」<sup>14</sup> 耶和华𢛟惶<u>摩西</u>，就话，「难道呒得<u>利未</u>人<u>亚伦</u>，尔个阿哥吗？我晓得其会讲说话。并且其走出来迎接尔。看见尔个时候，其心里会欢喜。」<sup>15</sup> 尔好等其讲，说话传拨其口里。我会帮助尔个嘴巴等其个嘴巴，并且教尔拉所当该做个事干。<sup>16</sup> <u>亚伦</u>会代尔话向百姓道。其会等尔代做嘴巴，尔会等其代做神明。<sup>17</sup> 尔手里好捏箇根拐杖用其做箇星奇事。

<sup>18</sup> Mo-si kyü-kyʽi, kyin gyi dziang-nying Yih-lo, teng gyi wô, Tsʽing ng, peh ngô cün-kyʽi kyin ngô hyüong-di, læ Yiæ-gyih di-fông-go, kʽen gyi-lah wa læ-kæn feh. Yih-lo teng Mo-si wô, Ng hao en-en w̆eng-w̆eng kyʽi. <sup>19</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô læ Mi-din teng Mo-si wô, Ng kyü Yiæ-gyih kyʽi, ing-we tang-sön ʽæ ng sing-ming-go nying tu si-de.

<sup>18</sup> <u>摩西</u>归去，见其丈人<u>叶罗</u>，等其话，「请尔，拨我转去见我兄弟，来<u>埃及</u>地方个，看其拉还来间弗。」<u>叶罗</u>等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔好安安稳稳去。」<sup>19</sup> 耶和华来<u>米甸</u>等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔归<u>埃及</u>去，因为打算害尔性命个人都死了。」

<sup>20</sup> Mo-si ta gyi tsʽi-ts ng-ts, peh gyi-lah gyi li-ts, kyü Yiæ-gyih di-fông kyʽi. Mo-si siu-li nyiah-leh Jing-ming-go kwa-dziang. <sup>21</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Ngô fông-læ ng siu-ʽô-go gyi-z, ng tao Yiæ-gyih-go z-ʽeo, tu pih-iao læ Fah-lao min-zin ka tso: dæn-z Ngô we ngang gyi-go sing, s-teh gyi feh-kʽeng fông pah-sing kyʽi. <sup>22</sup> Ng hao wô hyiang Fah-lao dao, Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Yi-seh-lih z Ngô ng-ts, ziu-z Ngô-go tsiang-ts. <sup>23</sup> Ngô teng ng wô, Fông Ngô ng-ts kyʽi hao voh-z Ngô: ng kyi-jün feh-kʽeng fông gyi, Ngô pih-ding sah-diao ng-go ng-ts, ziu-z ng-go tsiang-ts.

<sup>20</sup> <u>摩西</u>带其妻子儿子，拨其拉骑驴子，归<u>埃及</u>地方去。<u>摩西</u>手里捏勒神明个拐杖。<sup>21</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「我放来尔手下个奇事，尔到<u>埃及</u>个时候，都必要来<u>法老</u>面前介做。但是我会硬其个心，使得其弗肯放百姓去。」<sup>22</sup> 尔好话向<u>法老</u>道，耶和华是介话，「<u>以色列</u>是我儿子，就是我个长子。<sup>23</sup> 我等尔话，放我儿子去好服侍我。尔既然弗肯放其，我必定杀掉尔个儿子，就是尔个长子。」

<sup>24</sup> Kʽeo-kʽeo læ lu-zông ʽô-cʽü-li Yiæ-ʽo-wô pʽong-djoh Mo-si, iao sah gyi. <sup>25</sup> Si-pʽo-lah ziu do-leh ho-zah, keh gyi ng-ts ʽô-sing-go bi, tiu læ gyi kyiah-ʽô, wô, Ng jih-dzæ z ngô cʽih-hyüih-go dziang-fu. <sup>26</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ziu fông gyi kyʽi. Si-pʽo-lah yi wô, Ng z cʽih-hyüih-go dziang-fu, we-leh keh-go tsiu-keh-li.

<sup>24</sup> 扣扣来路上下处里耶和华碰着<u>摩西</u>，要杀其。<sup>25</sup> <u>西坡拉</u>就驮勒火石，割其儿子下身个皮，丢来其脚下，话，「尔实在是我出血个丈夫。」<sup>26</sup> 耶和华就放其去。<u>西坡拉</u>又话，「尔是出血个丈夫，为了箇个周割礼。」

<sup>27</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Üô-leng wô, Ng tao kwʽông-iæ kyʽi nying-tsih Mo-si. Gyi ziu kyʽi, nying-tsih gyi læ Jing-ming-go sæn-li, tsʽing gyi cü. <sup>28</sup> Mo-si pô Yiæ-ʽo-wô tsʽa gyi-go keh-sing shih-wô, teng feng-fu gyi-go keh-sing bing-kyü, tu wô hyiang Üô-leng dao.

<sup>27</sup> 耶和华等<u>亚伦</u>话，「尔到旷野去迎接<u>摩西</u>。」其就去，迎接其来神明个山里，请其嘴。<sup>28</sup> <u>摩西</u>把耶和华差其个箇星说话，等吩咐其个箇星凭据，都话向<u>亚伦</u>道。

<sup>29</sup> Mo-si teng Üô-leng kyʽi jü-long Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go cong tsiang-lao. <sup>30</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si sô kông-go shih-wô, Üô-leng tu djün-hyiang gyi-lah dao, yi læ pah-sing min-zin tso keh-sing bing-kyü. <sup>31</sup> Pah-sing ziu siang-sing: kyi-jün tʽing-meng Yiæ-ʽo-wô tsiao-kwun Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, kʽen-kwu gyi-lah-go kwʽu-tsʽu, ziu deo eo-tæn-tao pa-pa.

<sup>29</sup> <u>摩西</u>等<u>亚伦</u>去聚拢<u>以色列</u>子孙个众长老。<sup>30</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>所讲个说话，<u>亚伦</u>都传向其拉道，又来百姓面前做箇星凭据。<sup>31</sup> 百姓就相信。既然听闻耶和华照管<u>以色列</u>子孙，看顾其拉个苦楚，就头讴带倒拜拜。

### C. V.

ʽEO-DEO Mo-si teng Üô-leng tseo-tsing kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, teng gyi wô, Yi-seh-lih-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi hao læ kwʽông-iæ li teng ngô siu tsih-kʽeng. <sup>2</sup> Fah-lao wô, Yiæ-ʽo-wô z jü, iao s-teh ngô tʽing Gyi-go shih-wô, fông Yi-seh-lih pah-sing kyʽi ma? Ngô feh sih-teh Yiæ-ʽo-wô, yia feh-kʽeng fông Yi-seh-lih pah-sing kyʽi. <sup>3</sup> Gyi-lah wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming yi-kying we-djoh ah-lah. Tsʽing ng, cing ah-lah tao kwʽông-iæ-li tseo sæn-nyih-go lu-dzing kyʽi tsi ah-lah Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô, kʽong-pʽô Gyi kông-loh w̆eng-yüoh tao-tsʽiang kong-tang ah-lah. <sup>4</sup> Yiæ-gyih wông teng gyi-lah wô, Ng Mo-si teng Üô-leng, dza-we tsu-djü pah-sing-go sang-weh? Ng kyʽi, tʽiao ng-go tæn. <sup>5</sup> Fah-lao yi wô, Næn-kæn keh-go di-fông pah-sing z-ka to, ng-lah wa iao s-teh gyi-lah hyih-tæn.

后头<u>摩西</u>等<u>亚伦</u>走进去见<u>法老</u>，等其话，「<u>以色列</u>个神明耶和华是介话，放我百姓去好来旷野里等我守节肯。」<sup>2</sup> <u>法老</u>话，「耶和华是谁，要使得我听其个说话，放<u>以色列</u>百姓去吗？我弗识得耶和华，也弗肯放<u>以色列</u>百姓去。」<sup>3</sup> 其拉话，「<u>希伯来</u>人个神明已经会着阿拉。请尔，准阿拉到旷野里走三日个路程去祭阿拉神明耶和华，恐怕其降落瘟疫、刀枪攻打阿拉。」<sup>4</sup> <u>埃及</u>王等其拉话，「尔<u>摩西</u>等<u>亚伦</u>，咋会阻住百姓个生活？尔去，挑尔个担。」<sup>5</sup> <u>法老</u>又话，「难间箇个地方百姓是介多，尔拉还要使得其拉歇担。」

<sup>6</sup> Tông-nyih Fah-lao feng-fu pah-sing toh-kong-go teng gyi-lah tsoh-deo, wô, <sup>7</sup> Ng-lah feh-kʽo ziang zin-deo ka, do mah-ken peh pah-sing tso cün-deo: hao eo gyi-zi kyʽi siu mah-ken. <sup>8</sup> Zin-deo tso cün-deo-go su-moh, ng dzing-gyiu iao gyi tso-mun: ih-ngæn m-nao kæn-loh: ing-we gyi-lah læn-do; keh-lah gyiu-kʽeng wô, Peh ah-lah kyʽi tsi ah-lah Jing-ming. <sup>9</sup> Hao kô-tsʽeo keh-sing nying-go sang-weh, s-teh gyi-lah lao-kwʽu: feh kyʽi tsʽæ kʽong-deo shih-wô.

<sup>6</sup> 当日<u>法老</u>吩咐百姓督工个等其拉作头，话，<sup>7</sup> 「尔拉弗可像前头介，驮麦秆拨百姓做砖头。好讴其自去收麦秆。<sup>8</sup> 前头做砖头个数目，尔仍旧要其做满。一眼呒得好减落。因为其拉懒惰；箇拉求恳话，拨阿拉去祭阿拉神明。<sup>9</sup> 好加凑箇星人个生活，使得其拉劳苦。弗去睬空头说话。」

<sup>10</sup> Pah-sing toh-kong-go, teng gyi-lah tsoh-deo tseo-cʽih, teng pah-sing wô, Fah-lao z-ka wô, Ngô feh-kʽeng do mah-ken peh ng-lah. <sup>11</sup> Ng-lah kyʽi, ze ng-lah sô pʽong-djoh-go, zi siu mah-ken dæn-z ng-lah-go sang-weh ih-ngæn m-neh hao kæn-loh. <sup>12</sup> Z-ka pah-sing sæn-kʽæ læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông bah tsʽao, keng-cü tông mah-ken yüong. <sup>13</sup> Toh-kong-go tsʽe gyi-lah, wô, Ng-lah sang-weh iao djoh-nyih wun-kong, ziang zin-deo yiu mah-ken z-ʽeo ih-yiang. <sup>14</sup> Fah-lao toh-kong-go tang gyi sô shih-lih Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go tsoh-deo, meng gyi-lah wô, Zô-nyih teng kyih-mih, ng-lah sô tso-go cün-deo, dza-we m-neh tsiao-su wun-kong ziang zin-deo ka?

<sup>10</sup> 百姓督工个等其拉作头走出，等百姓话，「<u>法老</u>是介话，我弗肯驮麦秆拨尔拉。<sup>11</sup> 尔拉去，随尔拉所碰着个，自收麦秆。但是尔拉个生活一眼呒得好减落。」<sup>12</sup> 是介百姓散开来咸<u>埃及</u>地方拔草，根株当麦秆用。<sup>13</sup> 督工个催其拉，话，「尔拉生活要逐日完工，像前头有麦秆时候一样。」<sup>14</sup> <u>法老</u>督工个打其所设立<u>以色列</u>子孙个作头，问其拉话，「昨日等今末，尔拉所做个砖头，咋会呒得照数完工像前头介？」

<sup>15</sup> Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go tsoh-deo læ gyiu Fah-lao wô, Ng dza-we z-ka dæ nu-boh? <sup>16</sup> Gyi-lah feh do mah-ken peh ng nu-boh, yi feng-fu ah-lah tso cün-deo; ng-go nu-boh be tang; keh z ng-zi-go pah-sing ko-shih. <sup>17</sup> Fah-lao wô, Ng-lah læn-do, ng-lah læn-do; keh-lah wô, Peh ah-lah kyʽi tsi Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>18</sup> Næn-kæn ng-lah kyʽi, tso sang-weh; feh peh ng-lah mah-ken, dæn-z ng-lah tsong iao tsiao-su kao-dæ cün-deo.

<sup>15</sup> <u>以色列</u>子孙个作头来求<u>法老</u>话，「尔咋会是介待奴仆？<sup>16</sup> 其拉弗驮麦秆拨尔奴仆，又吩咐阿拉做砖头。尔个奴仆被打。箇是尔自个百姓过失。」<sup>17</sup> <u>法老</u>话，「尔拉懒惰，尔拉懒惰；箇拉话，拨阿拉去祭耶和华。」<sup>18</sup> 难间尔拉去，做生活。弗拨尔<u>拉麦</u>秆，但是尔拉总要照数交代砖头。

<sup>19</sup> Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go tsoh-deo tʽing-meng wô, Me-nyih tso-go cün-deo su-moh feh-kʽeng kæn-loh, ziu hyiao-teh gyi-zi ziu-ʽæ.

<sup>19</sup> <u>以色列</u>子孙个作头听闻话，「每日做个砖头数目弗肯减落」，就晓得其自受害。

<sup>20</sup> Li-kʽæ Fah-lao tseo-cʽih-go z-ʽeo, kʽeo-kʽeo pʽong-djoh Mo-si Üô-leng lu-ka-li lih-tong. <sup>21</sup> Ziu teng gyi-lah wô, Dæn-nyün Yiæ-ʽo-wô kʽen-kʽen ng-lah, pʽun-tön ng-lah, ing-we ng-lah s-teh ah-lah læ Fah-lao teng gyi dzing-ts-go ngæn-zin zah-tsʽiu-go, yi pô tao kao-fu gyi siu-li sah-diao ah-lah.

<sup>20</sup> 离开<u>法老</u>走出个时候，扣扣碰着<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>路街里立东。<sup>21</sup> 就等其拉话，「但愿耶和华看看尔拉，判断尔拉，因为尔拉使得阿拉来<u>法老</u>等其城子个眼前贼臭个，以把刀交付其手里杀掉阿拉。」

<sup>22</sup> Mo-si ziu tseo-cün, teng Yiæ-ʽo-wô wô, Cü, Ng dza-we ʽæ keh-go pah-sing dza-we tsʽa ngô? <sup>23</sup> Z dzong ngô kyin Fah-lao, kʽao-djoh Ng-go ming-deo, teng gyi kông ts-ʽeo, gyi yü-kô bao-nyiah keh-go pah-sing; Ng ih-ngæn m-neh kyiu Ng-go pah-sing.

<sup>22</sup> <u>摩西</u>就走转，等耶和华话，「主，尔咋会害箇个百姓咋会差我？<sup>23</sup> 自从我见<u>法老</u>，靠着尔个名头，等其讲之后，其愈加暴虐箇个百姓。尔一眼呒得救尔个百姓。」

### C. VI.

YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ teng Mo-si wô, Ngô sô iao tso-peh Fah-lao-go, ng pih-ding we kʽen-kyin; ing-we yüong neng-ken-go siu gyi we fông pah-sing kyʽi, ping-tsʽia yüong neng-ken-go siu gyi we ken gyi-lah cʽih gyi-go di-fông.

耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「我所要做拨<u>法老</u>个，尔必定会看见。因为用能干个手其会放百姓去，并且用能干个手其会赶其拉出其个地方。」

<sup>2</sup> Jing-ming yi teng Mo-si kông, z-ka wô, Ngô z YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ. <sup>3</sup> Zông-zin ngô yüong Djün-neng Jing-ming-go ming-deo hyin-cʽih peh Üô-pah-lah-hen, Yi-sah, Yüô-koh; dæn-z yüong Ngô-go ming-deo YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ, gyi-lah wa feh-hyiao-teh Ngô. <sup>4</sup> Ngô yi teng gyi-lah shih-lih Ngô-go iah, s-peh gyi-lah sô tæn-koh-go Kyüô-nen, ziu-z zin-deo gyi tso cʽih-meng nying-go di-fông. <sup>5</sup> Næn-kæn Yiæ-gyih nying bao-nyiah Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, Ngô yi-kying tʽing-meng gyi tʽæn-kyʽi: Ngô yia kyi-teh Ngô-go iah. <sup>6</sup> Sô-yi ng hao teng Yi-seh-lih ts-seng wô, Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô; Ngô we kyiu ng-lah tʽeh-cʽih Yiæ-gyih nying-go djong-tæn, fông ng-lah hyih-kong; yia we sing-cʽih siu læ, yüong djong-djong ying-vah, cʽü-joh ng-lah. <sup>7</sup> Ping-tsʽia Ngô kæn ng-lah tso Ngô-go pah-sing, Ngô tso ng-go Jing-ming: ka-ni ng we hyiao-teh Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô, ng-go Jing-ming, ziu-z kyiu ng tʽeh-cʽih Yiæ-gyih nying djong-tæn-go. <sup>8</sup> Ngô zin-deo sô vah-tsiu s-peh Üô-pah-lah-hen Yi-sah Yüô-koh-go di-fông, Ngô pih-ding ling ng-lah tseo-tao, peh ng-lah tso tsʽæn-nyih: Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>9</sup> Mo-si z-ka wô hyiang Yi-seh-lih ts-seng dao; dæn-z gyi-lah we-leh sing-li pe-sông ting kwʽu-go djong-tæn, feh tʽing-dzong Mo-si.

<sup>2</sup> 神明又等<u>摩西</u>讲，是介话，「我是耶和华。<sup>3</sup> 上前我用全能神明个名头显出拨<u>亚伯拉罕</u>、<u>以撒</u>、<u>雅各</u>；但是用我个名头耶和华，其拉还弗晓得我。<sup>4</sup> 我又等其拉设立我个约，赐拨其拉所耽搁个<u>迦南</u>，就是前头其做出门人个地方。<sup>5</sup> 难间<u>埃及</u>人暴虐<u>以色列</u>子孙，我已经听闻其叹气；我也记得我个约。<sup>6</sup> 所以尔好等<u>以色列</u>子孙话，我是耶和华。我会救尔拉脱出<u>埃及</u>人个重担，放尔拉歇工；也会伸出手来，用重重刑罚，取赎尔拉。<sup>7</sup> 并且我拣尔拉做我个百姓，我做尔个神明。介呢尔会晓得我是耶和华，尔个神明，就是救尔脱出<u>埃及</u>人重担个。<sup>8</sup> 我前头所罚咒赐拨<u>亚伯拉罕</u>、<u>以撒</u>、<u>雅各</u>个地方，我必定领尔拉走到，拨尔拉做产业。我是耶和华。」<sup>9</sup> <u>摩西</u>是介话向<u>以色列</u>子孙道。但是其拉为了心里悲伤顶苦个重担，弗听从<u>摩西</u>。

<sup>10</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô yi teng Mo-si wô, <sup>11</sup> Ng kyʽi tseo-ming Yiæ-gyih wông Fah-lao, eo gyi fông Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tseo-cʽih gyi-go di-fông. <sup>12</sup> Mo-si læ Yiæ-ʽo-wô min-zin wô, Yi-seh-lih ts-seng wa feh tʽing ngô, næn-dao Fah-lao kʽeng tʽing ngô, ziu-z cü-jing m-neh ziu tsiu-keh go nying ma? <sup>13</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si Üô-leng kông, eo gyi-lah feng-fu Yi-seh-lih nying teng Yiæ-gyih wông Fah-lao, hao ling Yi-seh-lih ts-seng cʽih Yiæ-gyih di-fông.

<sup>10</sup> 耶和华又等<u>摩西</u>话，<sup>11</sup> 「尔去奏明<u>埃及</u>王<u>法老</u>，讴其放<u>以色列</u>子孙走出其个地方。」<sup>12</sup> <u>摩西</u>来耶和华面前话，「<u>以色列</u>子孙还弗听我，难道<u>法老</u>肯听我，就是嘴唇呒得受周割个人吗？」<sup>13</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>讲，讴其拉吩咐<u>以色列</u>人等<u>埃及</u>王<u>法老</u>，好领<u>以色列</u>子孙出<u>埃及</u>地方。

<sup>14</sup> Keh-sing cong-dzoh li-hyiang tso deo-go læ ʽô-veng; Yi-seh-lih do ng-ts Liu-bin sô sang-go, Ha-noh, Fah-lu, Hyi-s-leng, Kyüô-mi. Keh-sing z Liu-bin-go vông-veng. <sup>15</sup> Si-min-go ng-ts, Yiæ-meo-li, Yüô-ming, O-ʽeh, Yüô-kying, So-ʽeh, teng Kyüô-nen nyü-nying sô sang-go Siao-lo. Keh-sing z Si-min-go vông-veng. <sup>16</sup> Li-vi ng-ts-go ming-z, tsiao gyi-lah shü-dæ, læ ʽô-veng; Keh-jing, Ko-ʽeh, Mi-lah-li. Li-vi-go ziu-shü ih-pah sæn-jih-tsʽih shü. <sup>17</sup> Keh-jing-go ng-ts, Lih-nyi, Z-me, tsiao gyi-lah vông-veng. <sup>18</sup> Ko-ʽeh-go ng-ts, En-læn, Yi-s-ha, Hyi-pah-leng, U-sih. Ko-ʽeh-go ziu-shü ih-pah sæn-jih-sæn shü. <sup>19</sup> Mi-lah-li-go ng-ts, Meh-li, Meo-z. Keh-sing z Li-vi-go ts-seng tsiao gyi-lah-go shü-dæ. <sup>20</sup> En-læn cʽü gyi ah-kwu Iah-kyi-bih tso tsʽi-ts; gyi sang Üô-leng; Mo-si peh En-læn. En-læn-go ziu-shü ih-pah sæn-jih-tsʽih shü. <sup>21</sup> Yi-s-ha-go ng-ts Kʽo-lah, Nyi-feh, Seh-li. <sup>22</sup> U-sih-go ng-ts, Mi-sô-li, Yi-li-sah-fæn, Seh-li. <sup>23</sup> Üô-leng cʽü Üô-mi-nô-dah-go nön, Nô-jing-go tsi-me, Yi-li-sô-pô tso tsʽi-ts; gyi sang Nô-tah, Üô-pi-wu, Yi-li-üô-sah, Yi-da-mô peh Üô-leng. <sup>24</sup> Kʽo-lah-go ng-ts, Üô-sih, Yi-li-kyüô-nô, Üô-pi-üô-sah: keh-sing z Kʽo-lah-go vông-veng. <sup>25</sup> Üô-leng-go ng-ts Yi-li-üô-sah cʽü Bu-tʽih nön li-hyiang ih-go tso tsʽi-ts; gyi sang Fi-nyi-ha. Keh-sing z Li-vi nying tsu-tsong tso deo-go, tsiao gyi-lah vông-veng. <sup>26</sup> Keh Üô-leng Mo-si z vong Yiæ-ʽo-wô ʽao-ling ling Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, tsiao gyi-lah-go ping-de cʽih Yiæ-gyih di-fông-go. <sup>27</sup> Teng Yiæ-gyih wông Fah-lao wô, Ling Yi-seh-lih ts-seng cʽih Yiæ-gyih-go, ziu-z keh-go Mo-si Üô-leng.

<sup>14</sup> 箇星众族里向做头个来下文。<u>以色列</u>大儿子<u>流便</u>所生个，<u>哈诺</u>、<u>法路</u>、<u>希斯伦</u>、<u>迦米</u>。箇星是<u>流便</u>个房份。<sup>15</sup> <u>西缅</u>个儿子，<u>耶母利</u>、<u>雅悯</u>、<u>阿辖</u>、<u>雅斤</u>、<u>琐辖</u>、等<u>迦南</u>女人所生个<u>少罗</u>。箇星是<u>西缅</u>个房份。<sup>16</sup> <u>利未</u>儿子个名字，照其拉世代，来下文。<u>革顺</u>、<u>哥辖</u>、<u>米拉利</u>。<u>利未</u>个寿岁一百三十七岁。<sup>17</sup> <u>革顺</u>个儿子，<u>立尼</u>、<u>示每</u>，照其拉房份。<sup>18</sup> <u>哥辖</u>个儿子，<u>暗兰</u>、<u>以斯哈</u>、<u>希伯仑</u>、<u>乌薛</u>。<u>哥辖</u>个寿岁一百三十三岁。<sup>19</sup> <u>米拉利</u>个儿子，<u>抹利</u>、<u>母示</u>。箇星是<u>利未</u>个子孙照其拉个世代。<sup>20</sup> <u>暗兰</u>娶其阿姑<u>约基别</u>做妻子。其生<u>亚伦</u>、<u>摩西</u>拨<u>暗兰</u>。<u>暗兰</u>个寿岁一百三十七岁。<sup>21</sup> <u>以斯哈</u>个儿子<u>可拉</u>、<u>尼弗</u>、<u>色利</u>。<sup>22</sup> <u>乌薛</u>个儿子，<u>米沙利</u>、<u>以利撒反</u>、<u>色利</u>。<sup>23</sup> <u>亚伦</u>娶<u>亚米拿达</u>个囡、<u>拿顺</u>个姊妹、<u>以利沙巴</u>做妻子。其生<u>拿搭</u>、<u>亚比户</u>、<u>以利亚撒</u>、<u>以大玛</u>拨<u>亚伦</u>。<sup>24</sup> <u>可拉</u>个儿子，<u>亚惜</u>、<u>以利加拿</u>、<u>亚比亚撒</u>。箇星是<u>可拉</u>个房份。<sup>25</sup> <u>亚伦</u>个儿子<u>以利亚撒</u>娶<u>部铁</u>囡里向一个做妻子。其生<u>非尼哈</u>。箇星是<u>利未</u>人祖宗做头个，照其拉房份。<sup>26</sup> 箇<u>亚伦</u>、<u>摩西</u>是奉耶和华号令领<u>以色列</u>子孙，照其拉个兵队出<u>埃及</u>地方个。<sup>27</sup> 等<u>埃及</u>王<u>法老</u>话，「领<u>以色列</u>子孙出<u>埃及</u>个，就是箇个<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>。」

<sup>28</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô læ Yiæ-gyih di-fông teng Mo-si kông-go z-ʽeo, <sup>29</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô feng-fu Mo-si wô, Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô; Ngô teng ng sô kông-go, ng tu wô hyiang Yiæ-gyih wông Fah-lao dao. <sup>30</sup> Mo-si læ Yiæ-ʽo-wô min-zin wô, Ngô-go cü-jing m-neh tsiu-keh; Fah-lao næn-dao kʽeng tʽing ngô ma?

<sup>28</sup> 耶和华来<u>埃及</u>地方等<u>摩西</u>讲个时候，<sup>29</sup> 耶和华吩咐<u>摩西</u>话，「我是耶和华。我等尔所讲个，尔都话向<u>埃及</u>王<u>法老</u>道。」<sup>30</sup> <u>摩西</u>来耶和华面前话，「我个嘴唇呒得周割。<u>法老</u>难道肯听我吗？」

### C. VII.

YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ teng Mo-si wô, Ng kʽen, Ngô shih-lih ng læ Fah-lao min-zin tso Jing-ming: Ng ah-ko Üô-leng we tso ng-go sin-cü-nying. <sup>2</sup> Væn-pah Ngô feng-fu ng go, ng tsong iao kông, ng ah-ko Üô-leng we wô hyiang Fah-lao dao, hao fông Yi-seh-lih ts-seng tseo-cʽih gyi-go di-ka. <sup>3</sup> Ngô we ngang Fah-lao-go sing, yia we tso hyü-to ziao-deo teng gyi-z læ Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>4</sup> Dæn-z Fah-lao pih-ding feh-kʽeng tʽing ng, s-teh Ngô! hao læ Yiæ-gyih dong-siu, yüong djong-djong ying-vah, ling Ngô-go ping-de, ziu-z ngô pah-sing Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, tseo-cʽih Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>5</sup> Ngô siu sing-cʽih læ Yiæ-gyih, ling Yi-seh-lih ts-seng cʽih gyi-lah cong-nyiang, ka z-ʽeo Yiæ-gyih nying we hyiao-teh Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>6</sup> Mo-si teng Üô-leng i Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go feng-fu, ziu-z-ka tso. <sup>7</sup> Teng Fah-lao kông-go z-ʽeo, Mo-si nyin-kyi yi-kying pah-jih shü, Üô-leng nyin-kyi pah-jih-sæn shü.

耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔看，我设立尔来<u>法老</u>面前做神明。尔阿哥<u>亚伦</u>会做尔个先知人。<sup>2</sup> 凡百我吩咐尔个，尔总要讲，尔阿哥<u>亚伦</u>会话向<u>法老</u>道，好放<u>以色列</u>子孙走出其个地界。<sup>3</sup> 我会硬<u>法老</u>个心，也会做许多兆头等奇事来<u>埃及</u>地方。<sup>4</sup> 但是<u>法老</u>必定弗肯听尔，使得我好来<u>埃及</u>动手，用重重刑罚，领我个兵队，就是我百姓<u>以色列</u>子孙，走出<u>埃及</u>地方。<sup>5</sup> 我手伸出来<u>埃及</u>，领<u>以色列</u>子孙出其拉中央，介时候<u>埃及</u>人会晓得我是耶和华。」<sup>6</sup> <u>摩西</u>等<u>亚伦</u>依耶和华个吩咐，就是介做。<sup>7</sup> 等<u>法老</u>讲个时候，<u>摩西</u>年纪已经八十岁，<u>亚伦</u>年纪八十三岁。

<sup>8</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô feng-fu Mo-si, wô, <sup>9</sup> Ziah-z Fah-lao teng ng-lah wô, Ng tso gyi-z; ka-ni ng hao teng Üô-leng wô, Do ng kwa-dziang, tiu læ Fah-lao min-zin; keh kwa-dziang ziu we pin tso dzô. <sup>10</sup> Mo-si Üô-leng tseo-tsing kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, tsiao Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go feng-fu ka tso: Üô-leng tiu kwa-dziang læ Fah-lao min-zin, teng gyi keh-sing dzing-ts min-zin: kwa-dziang ziu pin tso dzô. <sup>11</sup> Fah-lao ziu eo-long tsʽong-ming nying, teng ʽang zia-jih go; gyi-lah ziu-z Yiæ-gyih-go poh-lao, pô gyi-go fah-jih yia z-ka tso. <sup>12</sup> Koh-nying tiu gyi kwa-dziang, long-tsong pin tso dzô; dæn-z Üô-leng-go kwa-dziang tʽeng-kyʽüoh gyi-lah-go kwa-dziang. <sup>13</sup> Tsih-z Fah-lao-go sing ngang, feh-kʽeng tʽing gyi-lah, tsing-ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô sô kông ka.

<sup>8</sup> 耶和华吩咐<u>摩西</u>，话，<sup>9</sup> 「若是<u>法老</u>等尔拉话，尔做奇事。介呢尔好等<u>亚伦</u>话，驮尔拐杖，丢来<u>法老</u>面前。箇拐杖就会变做蛇。」<sup>10</sup> <u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>走进去见<u>法老</u>，照耶和华个吩咐介做。<u>亚伦</u>丢拐杖来<u>法老</u>面前，等其箇星臣子面前。拐杖就变做蛇。<sup>11</sup> <u>法老</u>就讴拢聪明人、等行邪术个。其拉就是<u>埃及</u>个博老，把其个法术也是介做。<sup>12</sup> 各人丢其拐杖，拢总变做蛇。但是<u>亚伦</u>个拐杖吞吃其拉个拐杖。<sup>13</sup> 只是<u>法老</u>个心硬，弗肯听其拉，正像耶和华所讲介。

<sup>14</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Fah-lao-go sing ngang, feh-kʽeng fông pah-sing kyʽi. <sup>15</sup> Ming-tsiao tʽin-nyiang ng kyʽi kyin Fah-lao: gyi we tseo-cʽih tao ʽo-yin; ng læ ʽo-pin-yin teng-ʽeo gyi, siu-li nyiah-leh zin-deo pin dzô-go kwa-dziang. <sup>16</sup> Ng hao teng gyi wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô tsʽa ngô kyin ng, z-ka wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi, hao læ kwʽông-iæ-li voh-z Ngô; tao jü-kying ng wa feh-kʽeng tʽing. <sup>17</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, We-leh keh-go z-ken ng hao hyiao-teh ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô: ngô pô siu-li nyiah-go kwa-dziang tang ʽo-li-go shü: shü pih-ding pin tso hyüih. <sup>18</sup> ʽO-li-go ng tu we si, ʽo yia we zah-tsʽiu: keh ʽo-li-go shü Yiæ-gyih nying pih-ding kyʽüoh-feh-læ. <sup>19</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Teng Üô-leng wô, Do ng kwa-dziang, sing-cʽih siu læ Yiæ-gyih keh-sing shü-go zông-deo; ziu-z gyi-lah kông, ʽo, dzi, teng væn-pah shü jü-long-go di-fông, s-teh gyi pin-tso hyüih; ping-tsʽia ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh jü-moh zah-deo kyʽi-ming cong-nyiang tao-cʽü yiu hyüih. <sup>20</sup> Mo-si Üô-leng tsiao Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go feng-fu ka tso; do-kyʽi kwa-dziang tang ʽo-li-go shü, læ Fah-lao min-zin, teng gyi dzing-ts-go min-zin: ʽo-li-go shü tu pin tso hyüih. <sup>21</sup> ʽO-li-go ng si-de: ʽo yia zah-tsʽiu-de; ʽo-li go shü Yiæ-gyih nying tu kyʽüoh-feh-læ: ping-tsʽia ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh tao-cʽü yiu hyüih. <sup>22</sup> Keh-tsao Yiæ-gyih-go poh-lao yüong gyi-lah fah-jih yia z-ka tso; Fah-lao-go sing ngang, feh-kʽeng tʽing gyi-lah, tsing-ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô sô kông ka-go. <sup>23</sup> Fah-lao tseo-cün wông-kong li-hyiang, yia feh liu-sing keh-go z-ken. <sup>24</sup> Yiæ-gyih nying, ing-we ʽo-li go shü kyʽüoh-feh-læ, keh-lah læ ʽo-pin-yin dön-kyʽün læ-kæn gyüih-loh-kyʽi, zing shü kyʽüoh. <sup>25</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô tang ʽo ts-ʽeo, ko-leh tsʽih nyih.

<sup>14</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「<u>法老</u>个心硬，弗肯放百姓去。<sup>15</sup> 明朝天亮尔去见<u>法老</u>。其会走出到河沿。尔来河边沿等候其，手里捏勒前头变蛇个拐杖。<sup>16</sup> 尔好等其话，<u>希伯来</u>人个神明耶和华差我见尔，是介话，放我百姓去，好来旷野里服侍我。到如今尔还弗肯听。<sup>17</sup> 耶和华是介话，为了箇个事干尔好晓得我是耶和华。我把手里捏个拐杖打河里个水。水必定变做血。<sup>18</sup> 河里个鱼都会死，河也会贼臭。箇河里个水<u>埃及</u>人必定吃弗来。」<sup>19</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「等<u>亚伦</u>话，驮尔拐杖，伸出手来<u>埃及</u>箇星水个上头，就是其拉江、河、池、等凡百水聚拢个地方，使得其变做血。并且咸<u>埃及</u>国树木石头器皿中央到处有血。」<sup>20</sup> <u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>照耶和华个吩咐介做。驮去拐杖打河里个水，来<u>法老</u>面前、等其臣子个面前。河里个水都变做血。<sup>21</sup> 河里个鱼死了。河也贼臭了。河里个水<u>埃及</u>人都吃弗来。并且咸<u>埃及</u>国到处有血。<sup>22</sup> 箇遭<u>埃及</u>个博老用其拉法术也是介做。<u>法老</u>个心硬，弗肯听其拉，正像耶和华所讲介个。<sup>23</sup> <u>法老</u>走转王宫里向，也弗留心箇个事干。<sup>24</sup> <u>埃及</u>人，因为河里个水吃弗来，箇拉来河边沿团圈来间掘落去，寻水吃。<sup>25</sup> 耶和华打河之后，过了七日。

### C. VIII.

YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ teng Mo-si wô, Ng kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, teng gyi wô, Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi, hao voh-z Ngô. <sup>2</sup> Ng ziah-z feh-kʽeng fông gyi-lah, Ngô pih-ding we s-teh tsʽing-w̆ô tsʽao-ziao ng dön-kyʽün-go di-ka. <sup>3</sup> ʽO-li we sang hyü-to tsʽing-w̆ô, zông-læ tseo-tsing ng oh-li, teng ng kwʽeng-go vông-li, ng-go min-zông keh-sing; yia tseo-tsing ng dzing-ts pah-sing-go oh-li, teng ng-go fong-lu: lin nyüoh min-feng-go kyʽi-ming. <sup>4</sup> Tsʽing-w̆ô yia we læ ng-go sing-zông, teng ng-go pah-sing lin long-tsong dzing-ts. <sup>5</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô feng-fu Mo-si, Teng Üô-leng wô, Ng nyiah-leh kwa-dziang, siu sing-cʽih læ kông, ʽo, dzi-go zông-teng, s-teh tsʽing-w̆ô tseo-zông Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>6</sup> Üô-leng ziu sing-cʽih siu læ Yiæ-gyih keh-sing shü-go zông-teng: tsʽing-w̆ô ziu tseo-zông, cʽong-mun ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>7</sup> Poh-lao yüong gyi fah-jih yia z-ka tso, s-teh tsʽing-w̆ô tseo-zông ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh. <sup>8</sup> Keh-tsao Fah-lao eo Mo-si Üô-leng, wô, Ng gyiu Yiæ-ʽo-wô, djü-diao tsʽing-w̆ô li-kʽæ ngô, teng ngô-go pah-sing; ngô we fông-cʽih pah-sing kyʽi tsi Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>9</sup> Mo-si teng Fah-lao wô, Tsʽing ng, ding peh ngô soh-go z-ʽeo hao we-leh ng, teng ng dzing-ts pah-sing, gyiu Gyi djü-diao tsʽing-w̆ô li-kʽæ ng, teng ng oh-li, s-teh tæn-tsih læ ʽo-li dzing-loh-tong. <sup>10</sup> Gyi wô, Ming-tsiao. Mo-si wô, Tsiao ng-go shih-wô hao s-teh; peh ng hyiao-teh m-yiu ziang ah-lah-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô ka-go. <sup>11</sup> Tsʽing-w̆ô pih-ding we li-kʽæ ng, teng ng oh-li, lin ng-go dzing-ts, ng-go pah-sing; tæn-tsih læ ʽo-li dzing-loh-tong. <sup>12</sup> Mo-si teng Üô-leng li-kʽæ Fah-lao tseo-cʽih: Mo-si gyiu-kʽeng Yiæ-ʽo-wô we-leh Gyi sô ying tao Fah-lao di-fông-go tsʽing-w̆ô. <sup>13</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô tsiao Mo-si sô kông ka tso: væn-pah læ oh-li, læ ming-dông, læ din-li-go tsʽing-w̆ô, long-tsong si-de. <sup>14</sup> Cong-nying siu-gyi-long te-tang-te; keh di-fông tu zah-tsʽiu-de. <sup>15</sup> Fah-lao kʽen-kyin yi-kying kwʽun-s-de, ziu ngang zi-go sing, feh-kʽeng tʽing-dzong gyi-lah: ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô sô wô ka-go.

耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔去见<u>法老</u>，等其话，耶和华是介话，放我百姓去，好服侍我。<sup>2</sup> 尔若是弗肯放其拉，我必定会使得青蛙吵扰尔团圈个地界。<sup>3</sup> 河里会生许多青蛙，上来走进尔屋里、等尔困个房里、尔个眠床箇星；也走进尔臣子百姓个屋里、等尔个风炉、连肉面粉个器皿。<sup>4</sup> 青蛙也会来尔个身上、等尔个百姓、连拢总臣子。」<sup>5</sup> 耶和华吩咐<u>摩西</u>，「等<u>亚伦</u>话，尔捏勒拐杖，手伸出来江、河、池个上顶，使得青蛙走上<u>埃及</u>地方。」<sup>6</sup> <u>亚伦</u>就伸出手来<u>埃及</u>箇星水个上顶。青蛙就走上，充满咸<u>埃及</u>地方。<sup>7</sup> 博老用其法术也是介做，使得青蛙走上咸<u>埃及</u>国。<sup>8</sup> 箇遭<u>法老</u>讴<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>，话，「尔求耶和华，除掉青蛙离开我，等我个百姓。我会放出百姓去祭耶和华。」<sup>9</sup> <u>摩西</u>等<u>法老</u>话，「请尔，定拨我啥个时候好为了尔、等尔臣子百姓，求其除掉青蛙离开尔、等尔屋里，使得单只来河里剩落东。」<sup>10</sup> 其话，「明朝。」<u>摩西</u>话，「照尔个说话好使得。拨尔晓得呒有像阿拉个神明耶和华介个。<sup>11</sup> 青蛙必定会离开尔、等尔屋里、连尔个臣子、尔个百姓。单只来河里剩落东。」<sup>12</sup> <u>摩西</u>等<u>亚伦</u>离开<u>法老</u>走出。<u>摩西</u>求恳耶和华，为了其所引到<u>法老</u>地方个青蛙。<sup>13</sup> 耶和华照<u>摩西</u>所讲介做。凡百来屋里、来明堂、来田里个青蛙，拢总死了。<sup>14</sup> 众人收其拢堆打堆。箇地方都贼臭了。<sup>15</sup> <u>法老</u>看见已经宽弛了，就硬自个心，弗肯听从其拉，像耶和华所话介个。

<sup>16</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô feng-fu Mo-si, Teng Üô-leng wô, Do-kyʽi ng-go kwa-dziang, tang di-tʽu-go hwe-dzing, s-teh gyi pin-tso seh læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>17</sup> Gyi-lah ziu-z-ka tso; Üô-leng do-leh kwa-dziang sing-cʽih siu læ, tang di-tʽu-go hwe-dzing, ziu pin-tso seh læ nying teng sang-kʽeo-go sing-zông: keh di-tʽu-go hwe-dzing tu pin-tso seh læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>18</sup> Poh-lao yüong fah-jih yia z-ka tso, iao pin-tso seh, pin-feh-læ: z-ka yiu seh læ nying teng sang-kʽeo-go sing-zông. <sup>19</sup> Poh-lao teng Fah-lao wô, Keh z Jing-ming-go siu-dön: Fah-lao sing ngang, feh-kʽeng tʽing-dzong gyi-lah, ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô sô wô ka-go.

<sup>16</sup> 耶和华吩咐<u>摩西</u>，「等<u>亚伦</u>话，驮起尔个拐杖，打地土个灰尘，使得其变做虱来咸<u>埃及</u>地方。」<sup>17</sup> 其拉就是介做。<u>亚伦</u>驮勒拐杖伸出手来，打地土个灰尘，就变做虱来人等牲口个身上。箇地土个灰尘都变做虱来咸<u>埃及</u>地方。<sup>18</sup> 博老用法术也是介做，要变做虱，变弗来。是介有虱来人等牲口个身上。<sup>19</sup> 博老等<u>法老</u>话，「箇是神明个手段。」<u>法老</u>心硬，弗肯听从其拉，像耶和华所话介个。

<sup>20</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Ng ming-tsiao tsao tʽin-nyiang kyʽi-læ, Fah-lao we tseo-cʽih tao shü pin-yin. Ng lih-læ gyi min-zin, teng gyi wô, Yiæ-ʽo-wô wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi hao voh-z Ngô. <sup>21</sup> Ziah-z feh-kʽeng fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi, Ngô pih-ding tsʽa dziao tang dziao tsʽông-ing ʽæ ng, teng ng dzing-ts pah-sing, lin ng-go oh-li: keh-sing dziao tang dziao tsʽông-ing, we cʽong-mun Yiæ-gyih nying-go oh-li, teng gyi-lah sô læ-kæn-go di-fông. <sup>22</sup> Keh-nyih Ngô we feng-pih Ngô pah-sing sô deng-kæn-go Ko-sæn di-fông: læ keh-deo m-neh tsʽông-ing, s-teh ng hao hyiao-teh læ ʽen-tʽin-ʽô cong-nyiang Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>23</sup> Ngô we feng-pih ng-go pah-sing teng Ngô-go pah-sing: ming-tsiao pih ding yiu keh-go ziao-deo. <sup>24</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ziu-z ka tso; ziu yiu hyü-to dziao-su tsʽông-ing fi-tsing Fah-lao oh-li, teng gyi dzing-ts-go oh-li, lin ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh: keh di-fông we-leh dziao tang dziao tsʽông-ing wæ-diao-de. <sup>25</sup> Fah-lao eo Mo-si Üô-leng, wô, Ng kyʽi, hao læ tsʽ-di tsi ng-go Jing-ming. <sup>26</sup> Mo-si wô, Feh kʽo-yi ka tso: ing-we ah-lah yüong Yiæ-gyih nying kʽo-u-go tong-si tsi ah-lah Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô. Ziah-z ah-lah læ Yiæ-gyih nying-go ngæn-zin yüong gyi kʽo-u-go tong-si hyin-tsi, gyi-lah næn-dao feh do zah-deo kʽang ah-lah ma? <sup>27</sup> Ah-lah tsong iao tao kwʽông-iæ-li tseo-leh sæn-nyih-go lu-dzing, tsi ah-lah-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô, tsiao gyi sô feng-fu ah-lah-go. <sup>28</sup> Fah-lao wô, Ngô we fông ng kyʽi tao kwʽông-iæ-li tsi ng-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô; tsih-z ng feh-kʽo tseo-yün. Tsʽing ng, we-leh ngô gyiu-gyiu. <sup>29</sup> Mo-si wô, Næn-kæn ngô li-kʽæ ng, tseo-cʽih-kyʽi gyiu Yiæ-ʽo-wô, s-teh dziao tang dziao tsʽông-ing ming-tsiao li-kʽæ Fah-lao, yia li-kʽæ ng-go dzing-ts pah-sing: dæn-z Fah-lao m-nao tsæ hong-pʽin, feh-kʽeng fông pah-sing kyʽi tsi Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>30</sup> Mo-si ziu tseo-cʽih li-kʽæ Fah-lao, kyʽi gyiu Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>31</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô i Mo-si shih-wô ka tso; djü-diao dziao tang dziao tsʽông-ing li-kʽæ Fah-lao, yia li-kʽæ gyi dzing-ts pah-sing; m-yiu ih-go dzing-loh. <sup>32</sup> Ka z-ʽeo Fah-lao dzing-gyiu ngang zi-go sing, feh-kʽeng fông pah-sing kyʽi.

<sup>20</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔明朝早天亮起来，<u>法老</u>会走出到水边沿。尔立来其面前，等其话，耶和华话，放我百姓去好服侍我。<sup>21</sup> 若是弗肯放我百姓去，我必定差潮打潮苍蝇害尔、等尔臣子百姓、连尔个屋里。箇星潮打潮苍蝇，会充满<u>埃及</u>人个屋里、等其拉所来间个地方。<sup>22</sup> 箇日我会分别我百姓所庉间个<u>歌珊</u>地方。来箇头呒得苍蝇，使得尔好晓得来咸天下中央我是耶和华。<sup>23</sup> 我会分别尔个百姓等我个百姓。明朝必定有箇个兆头。」<sup>24</sup> 耶和华就是介做。就有许多潮数苍蝇飞进<u>法老</u>屋里、等其臣子个屋里、连咸<u>埃及</u>国。箇地方为了潮打潮苍蝇坏掉了。<sup>25</sup> <u>法老</u>讴<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>，话，「尔去，好来此地祭尔个神明。」<sup>26</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「弗可以介做。因为阿拉用<u>埃及</u>人可恶个东西祭阿拉神明耶和华。若是阿拉来<u>埃及</u>人个眼前用其可恶个东西献祭，其拉难道弗驮石头揞阿拉吗？<sup>27</sup> 阿拉总要到旷野里走勒三日个路程，祭阿拉个神明耶和华，照其所吩咐阿拉个。」<sup>28</sup> <u>法老</u>话，「我会放尔去到旷野里祭尔个神明耶和华。只是尔弗可走远。请尔，为了我求求。」<sup>29</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「难间我离开尔，走出去求耶和华，使得潮打潮苍蝇明朝离开<u>法老</u>，也离开尔个臣子百姓。但是<u>法老</u>呒得好再哄骗，弗肯放百姓去祭耶和华。」<sup>30</sup> <u>摩西</u>就走出离开<u>法老</u>，去求耶和华。<sup>31</sup> 耶和华依<u>摩西</u>说话介做。除掉潮打潮苍蝇离开<u>法老</u>，也离开其臣子百姓。呒有一个剩落。<sup>32</sup> 介时候<u>法老</u>仍旧硬自个心，弗肯放百姓去。

### C. IX.

YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ teng Mo-si wô, Ng tseo-kyʽi kyin Fah-lao, teng gyi wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi hao voh-z Ngô. <sup>2</sup> Ziah-z feh-kʽeng fông, dzing-gyiu ngang-liu gyi-lah, <sup>3</sup> ka-ni, Yiæ-ʽo-wô we dong-siu læ ng din-li sang-kʽeo-go sing-zông, ziu-z mô, li-ts, loh-do, ngeo, yiang keh-sing; pih-ding yiu djong-djong-go w̆eng-yüoh. <sup>4</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô we feng-pih? Yi-seh-lih-go sang-kʽeo teng Yiæ-gyih-go sang-kʽeo: væn-pah joh-ü Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go tu feh we si. <sup>5</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ziu ding ih-go nyih-ts, wô, Ming-tsiao Yiæ-ʽo-wô we læ tsʽ-di tso keh-go z-ken. 6Tao di-nyi-nyih Yiæ-ʽo-wô ziu-z ka tso; Yiæ-gyih keh-sing sang-kʽeo long-tsong si-de; tsih-yiu Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go sang-kʽeo ih-tsah tu m-neh si. <sup>7</sup> Fah-lao tsʽa nying kyʽi kʽen Yi-seh-lih ts-seng-go sang-kʽeo, ko-jün ih-tsah tu m-neh si: dæn-z Fah-lao dzing-gyiu sing ngang, feh-kʽeng fông pah-sing kyʽi.

耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔走去见<u>法老</u>，等其话，<u>希伯来</u>人个神明耶和华是介话，放我百姓去好服侍我。<sup>2</sup> 若是弗肯放，仍旧硬留其拉，<sup>3</sup> 介呢，耶和华会动手来尔田里牲口个身上，就是马、驴子、骆驼、牛、羊箇星。必定有重重个瘟疫。<sup>4</sup> 耶和华会分别<u>以色列</u>个牲口等<u>埃及</u>个牲口。凡百属于<u>以色列</u>子孙个都弗会死。」<sup>5</sup> 耶和华就定一个日子，话，「明朝耶和华会来此地做箇个事干。」<sup>6</sup> 到第二日耶和华就是介做。<u>埃及</u>箇星牲口拢总死了。只有<u>以色列</u>子孙个牲口一只都呒得死。<sup>7</sup> <u>法老</u>差人去看<u>以色列</u>子孙个牲口，果然一只都呒得死。但是<u>法老</u>仍旧心硬，弗肯放百姓去。

<sup>8</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si Üô-leng wô, Ng-lah do fong-lu-li hwe ih-pô, s-teh Mo-si læ Fah-lao min-zin, dziao tʽin sæn-kʽæ-kyʽi; <sup>9</sup> Ziu we pin-tso hwe-dzing læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông: læ Yiæ-gyih koh dön-kyʽün-go nying teng sang-kʽeo-go sing-zông fah-cʽih tsʽông-doh læ. <sup>10</sup> Gyi-lah ziu do fong-lu-li hwe, lih-læ Fah-lao min-zin; Mo-si dziao tʽin sæn-kʽæ-kyʽi: ziu yiu tsʽông-doh fah-cʽih læ nying teng sang-kʽeo-go sing-zông. <sup>11</sup> Poh-lao we-leh sang tsʽông-go yün-kwu, feh-neng-keo lih-læ Mo-si min-zin: ing-we poh-lao teng Yiæ-gyih-go cong-nying tu sang tsʽông-doh. <sup>12</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ngang Fah-lao-go sing, s-teh feh-kʽeng tʽing gyi-lah, ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si sô kông ka-go.

<sup>8</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>话，「尔拉驮风炉里灰一把，使得<u>摩西</u>来<u>法老</u>面前，朝天散开去。<sup>9</sup> 就会变做灰尘来咸<u>埃及</u>地方。来<u>埃及</u>国团圈个人等牲口个身上发出疮毒来。」<sup>10</sup> 其拉就驮风炉里灰，立来<u>法老</u>面前。<u>摩西</u>朝天散开去。就有疮毒发出来人等牲口个身上。<sup>11</sup> 博老为了生疮个缘故，弗能够立来<u>摩西</u>面前。因为博老等<u>埃及</u>个众人都生疮毒。<sup>12</sup> 耶和华硬<u>法老</u>个心，使得弗肯听其拉，像耶和华等<u>摩西</u>所讲介个。

<sup>13</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Tsao tʽin-nyiang kyʽi-læ, lih-læ Fah-lao min-zin, teng gyi wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi hao voh-z Ngô. <sup>14</sup> Ngô yin-dzæ we kông-loh hyü-to tsæ-næn læ ng-go sing-li, teng ng-go dzing-ts, ng-go pah-sing, peh ng hao hyiao-teh pʽu-tʽin-ʽô m-yiu ziang Ngô ka-go. <sup>15</sup> Ngô feh-jün iao sing-cʽih siu læ, yüong z-yüoh bing tang ng, teng ng-go pah-sing: ng pih-ding we dzong di-yiang-li djü-mih-diao: <sup>16</sup> dæn-z Ngô sô-yi peh ng hying-kyʽi-læ, z deh-we iao hyiang ng hyin-cʽih Ngô-go neng-ken, yi hao læ pin-tʽin-ʽô yiang-kʽæ Ngô-go ming-sing. <sup>17</sup> Næn-kæn ng wa we zi kyiao ngao tsu-kah Ngô-go pah-sing feh-kʽeng fông gyi-lah kyʽi ma? <sup>18</sup> Ng kʽen, ming-tsiao keh-go z-ʽeo, Ngô we loh ting djong-go boh-ts; dzong Yiæ-gyih kʽæ-koh tao næn-kæn, m-yiu ziang keh-cong-ka. <sup>19</sup> Keh-lah ng kyʽi tsʽa nying siu-tsing ng-go sang-kʽeo, teng din-li ih-tsʽih sô-yiu-go: ing-we væn-pah nying teng sang-kʽeo læ din-deo, feh-zing siu-tsing oh-li-go, boh-ts we loh læ gyi sing-zông, ziu tang-sah gyi-lah. <sup>20</sup> Fah-lao dzing-ts cong-nyiang pʽô-gyü Yiæ-ʽo-wô shih-wô go, koh-nying eo gyi nu-boh teng sang-kʽeo to-bi læ oh-li. <sup>21</sup> Tsih-yiu feh liu-sing Yiæ-ʽo-wô shih-wô go, dzing-loh gyi nu-boh teng sang-kʽeo læ din-li. <sup>22</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Sing-cʽih ng-go siu dziao-leh tʽin, s-teh ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh boh-ts loh læ nying, teng sang-kʽeo, teng din-li keh-sing su-tsʽæ-go zông-teng, ziu-z væn-pah læ Yiæ-gyih di-fông go. <sup>23</sup> Mo-si ziu di-kyʽi kwa-dziang hyiang-djoh tʽin: Yiæ-ʽo-wô fah-cʽih le teng boh-ts; di-yiang-li ho-kwông sin-læ sin-kyʽi: z-ka Yiæ-ʽo-wô loh boh-ts læ Yiæ-gyih di-fông. <sup>24</sup> Ziu yiu boh-ts, boh-ts kah-leh ho-kwông, pʽô-shü-shü-go; dzong kʽæ-koh yi-læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông m-yiu ziang keh-cong-ka. <sup>25</sup> ʽEn Yiæ-gyih koh læ din-li-go nying teng sang-kʽeo, boh-ts long-tsong tang gyi-lah: yi tang-diao din-li-go pah-yiang su-tsʽæ, ao-dön din-li-go pah-yiang jü-moh. <sup>26</sup> Tsih-yiu? Yi-seh-lih ts-seng deng-kæn-go Ko-sæn di-fông m-neh boh-ts. <sup>27</sup> Fah-lao tsʽa nying, eo Mo-si Üô-leng, teng gyi-lah wô, Ngô keh-tsao yiu ze: Yiæ-ʽo-wô z tsing-dzih-go: ngô teng ngô-go pah-sing z ôh-go. <sup>28</sup> Ka keo-de; tsʽing ng gyiu Yiæ-ʽo-wô, m-nao tsæ fah do-do le teng boh-ts; ngô we fông ng-lah kyʽi, feh tsæ liu ng-lah. <sup>29</sup> Mo-si teng gyi wô, Ngô tseo-cʽih dzing-li, di-kyʽi ngô-go siu hyiang-djoh Yiæ-ʽo-wô; le ziu we feh hyiang; boh-ts feh-we tsæ loh; s-teh ng hao hyiao-teh di z joh-ü Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go. <sup>30</sup> Dæn-z ng teng ng-go dzing-ts, ngô hyiao-teh feh-kʽeng pʽô-gyü Yiæ-ʽo-wô Jing-ming. <sup>31</sup> Ka z-ʽeo mô teng do-mah tu tang-diao-de: ing-we do-mah yi-kying kyih-be: mô yi-kying kʽæ-hwô-de. <sup>32</sup> Tsih-yiu siao-mah teng tsʽu-mah m-neh tang-diao, ing-we gyi feh-zing fah-cʽih-læ. <sup>33</sup> Mo-si li-kʽæ Fah-lao tseo-cʽih dzing-nga; di-kyʽi! siu hyiang-djoh Yiæ-ʽo-wô, le teng boh-ts ziu hyih: yü yia feh tao-loh di-li. <sup>34</sup> Fah-lao kʽen-kyin yü, boh-ts, le yi-kying hyih-de, ziu yü-kô væn-ze, ngang zi-go sing; gyi-zi teng dzing-ts tu z-ka. <sup>35</sup> Ka-ni Fah-lao sing ngang, feh-kʽeng fông Yi-seh-lih ts-seng, ziang Yiæ-ʽo-wô tʽoh Mo-si sô kông ka-go.

<sup>13</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「早天亮起来，立来<u>法老</u>面前，等其话，<u>希伯来</u>人个神明耶和华是介话，放我百姓去好服侍我。<sup>14</sup> 我现在会降落许多灾难来尔个心里、等尔个臣子、尔个百姓，拨尔好晓得普天下呒有像我介个。<sup>15</sup> 我弗然要伸出手来，用时疫病打尔、等尔个百姓。尔必定会从地垟里除灭掉。<sup>16</sup> 但是我所以拨尔兴起来，是特为要向尔显出我个能干，又好来遍天下扬开我个名声。<sup>17</sup> 难间尔还会自骄傲阻隔我个百姓弗肯放其拉去吗？<sup>18</sup> 尔看，明朝箇个时候，我会落顶重个雹子。从<u>埃及</u>开国到难间，呒有像箇种介。<sup>19</sup> 箇拉尔去差人收进尔个牲口、等田里一切所有个。因为凡百人等牲口来田头，弗曾收进屋里个，雹子会落来其身上，就打煞其拉。」<sup>20</sup> <u>法老</u>臣子中央怕惧耶和华说话个，各人讴其奴仆等牲口躲避来屋里。<sup>21</sup> 只有弗留心耶和华说话个，剩落其奴仆等牲口来田里。<sup>22</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「伸出尔个手朝勒天，使得咸<u>埃及</u>国雹子落来人、等牲口、等田里箇星蔬菜个上顶，就是凡百来<u>埃及</u>地方个。」<sup>23</sup> <u>摩西</u>就提起拐杖向着天。耶和华发出雷等雹子。地垟里火光<u>闪</u>来<u>闪</u>去。是介耶和华落雹子来<u>埃及</u>地方。<sup>24</sup> 就有雹子，雹子夹勒火光，怕势势个。从开国以来咸<u>埃及</u>地方呒有像箇种介。<sup>25</sup> 咸<u>埃及</u>国来田里个人等牲口，雹子拢总打其拉。又打掉田里个百样蔬菜，拗断田里个百样树木。<sup>26</sup> 只有<u>以色列</u>子孙庉间个<u>歌珊</u>地方呒得雹子。<sup>27</sup> <u>法老</u>差人，讴<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>，等其拉话，「我箇遭有罪。耶和华是正直个。我等我个百姓是恶个。<sup>28</sup> 介够了。请尔求耶和华，呒得好再发大大雷等雹子。我会放尔拉去，弗再留尔拉。」<sup>29</sup> <u>摩西</u>等其话，「我走出城里，提起我个手向着耶和华。雷就会弗响。雹子弗会再落。使得尔好晓得地是属于耶和华个。<sup>30</sup> 但是尔等尔个臣子，我晓得弗肯怕惧耶和华神明。」<sup>31</sup> 介时候麻等大麦都打掉了。因为大麦已经结佩。麻已经开花了。<sup>32</sup> 只有小麦等粗麦呒得打掉，因为其弗曾发出来。<sup>33</sup> <u>摩西</u>离开<u>法老</u>走出城外。提起手向着耶和华。雷等雹子就歇。雨也弗倒落地里。<sup>34</sup> <u>法老</u>看见雨、雹子、雷已经歇了，就愈加犯罪，硬自个心。其自等臣子都是介。<sup>35</sup> 介呢<u>法老</u>心硬，弗肯放<u>以色列</u>子孙，像耶和华托<u>摩西</u>所讲介个。

### C. X.

YIÆ-ʽO-WÔ teng Mo-si wô, Ng tseo-tsing kyʽi kyin Fah-lao; ing-we Ngô yi-kying ngang gyi-go sing teng dzing-ts-go sing, hao hyin-cʽih Ngô-go ziao-deo læ gyi-go cong-nyiang; <sup>2</sup> s-teh ng pô Ngô læ Yiæ-gyih sô-tso-go z-ken, læ gyi-lah cong-nyiang sô hyin-cʽih-go ziao-deo, djün peh ng-go ts-seng ng-tô tʽing-meng; hao hyiao-teh Ngô z Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>3</sup> Mo-si Üô-leng tseo-tao Fah-lao di-fông, teng gyi wô, Hyi-pah-læ nying-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô z-ka wô, Ng feh-kʽeng ti-sing siao-i læ Ngô-go min-zin, tao kyi-z ni? yüong fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi hao voh-z Ngô. <sup>4</sup> Ziah-z feh-kʽeng fông Ngô pah-sing kyʽi, ming-tsiao Ngô we s-teh wông-djong tseo-tsing ng-go di-ka: <sup>5</sup> gyi-lah we tsô-djü di min-teng, s-teh di-yiang tu kʽen-feh-kyin, yi we kyʽüoh dzing-loh peh ng-lah-go tong-si, ziu-z boh-ts sô feh-zing wæ-diao-go; wa-yiu kyʽüoh din-li sô sang peh ng-lah-go jü-moh. <sup>6</sup> Gyi-lah we cʽong-mun! ng-go oh-li, teng ng cong dzing-ts-go oh-li, lin Yiæ-gyih cong pah-sing-go oh-li: ziu-z ng ah-tia teng tsu-tsong, dzong cʽih-si yi-læ tao jü-kying sô feh-zing kʽen-kyin-ko-go. Mo-si ziu nyin-cün, li-kʽæ Fah-lao tseo-cʽih. <sup>7</sup> Fah-lao-go dzing-ts teng gyi wô, Keh-go nying ʽæ ah-lah tao kyi-z ni? Tsʽing ng, fông keh-sing nying kyʽi, hao voh-z gyi-lah Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô: Yiæ-gyih yi-kying mih-diao-de, ng wa we feh-hyiao-teh ma? <sup>8</sup> Yi ling Mo-si Üô-leng tao Fah-lao di-fông. Fah-lao teng gyi-lah wô, Kyʽi voh-z ng-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô; dæn-z kyʽi-go nying z jü ni? <sup>9</sup> Mo-si wô, Ah-lah lin lao-lao siao-siao, ng-ts nön teng ah-lah ngeo yiang dô-kô kyʽi: ing-we ah-lah iao siu Yiæ-ʽo-wô-go tsih-kʽeng. <sup>10</sup> Fah-lao teng gyi-lah wô, Dæn-nyün Yiæ-ʽo-wô pao-yiu ng-lah, ziang ngô fông ng-lah teng ng siao-nying dô-kô kyʽi ka: ng-lah yüong siao-sing; ʽo-se læ ng-lah min-zin-de, <sup>11</sup> Feh-z-ka: ng-lah do-nying kyʽi voh-z Yiæ-ʽo-wô: keh z ng-lah ih-hyiang sô gyiu-go: gyi-lah ziu be ken-cʽih li-kʽæ Fah-lao min-zin.

耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔走进去见<u>法老</u>。因为我已经硬其个心等臣子个心，好显出我个兆头来其个中央。<sup>2</sup> 使得尔把我来<u>埃及</u>所做个事干，来其拉中央所显出个兆头，传拨尔个子孙耳朵听闻。好晓得我是耶和华。」<sup>3</sup> <u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>走到<u>法老</u>地方，等其话，「<u>希伯来</u>人个神明耶和华是介话，尔弗肯低声小意来我个面前，到几时呢？用放我百姓去好服侍我。<sup>4</sup> 若是弗肯放我百姓去，明朝我会使得蝗虫走进尔个地界。<sup>5</sup> 其拉会遮住地面顶，使得地垟都看弗见，又会吃剩落拨尔拉个东西，就是雹子所弗曾坏掉个；还有吃田里所生拨尔拉个树木。<sup>6</sup> 其拉会充满尔个屋里、等尔众臣子个屋里、连<u>埃及</u>众百姓个屋里。就是尔阿爹等祖宗，从出世以来到如今所弗曾看见过个。」<u>摩西</u>就扭转，离开<u>法老</u>走出。<sup>7</sup> <u>法老</u>个臣子等其话，「箇个人害阿拉到几时呢？请尔，放箇星人去，好服侍其拉神明耶和华。<u>埃及</u>已经灭掉了，尔还会弗晓得吗？」<sup>8</sup> 又领<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>到<u>法老</u>地方。<u>法老</u>等其拉话，「去服侍尔个神明耶和华。但是去个人是谁呢？」<sup>9</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「阿拉连老老少少、儿子囡、等阿拉牛羊大家去。因为阿拉要守耶和华个节肯。」<sup>10</sup> <u>法老</u>等其拉话，「但愿耶和华保佑尔拉，像我放尔拉等尔小人大家去介。尔拉用小心。祸祟来尔拉面前了。<sup>11</sup> 弗是介。尔拉大人去服侍耶和华。箇是尔拉一向所求个。」其拉就被赶出离开<u>法老</u>面前。

<sup>12</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Sing-cʽih? siu læ Yiæ-gyih koh, s-teh wông-djong tseo-tsing Yiæ-gyih di-ka, kyʽüoh keh di-zông pah-yiang su-tsʽæ; ziu-z boh-ts sô dzing-loh-tong-go. <sup>13</sup> Mo-si ziu di-kyʽi kwa-dziang læ Yiæ-gyih koh; Yiæ-ʽo-wô s-teh tong-fong* tsing-nyih tsing-yia cʽü keh di-fông: tao tʽin-nyiang tong-fong ta-leh wông-djong tseo-tsing. <sup>14</sup> Wông-djong sæn-kʽæ Yiæ-gyih pin-di, ding-læ Yiæ-gyih dön-kyʽün di-ka li-hyiang; z ting li-ʽæ; dzong-zin m-yiu keh-cong-ka wông-djong, yi-ʽeo yia tsæ m-yiu keh-cong-ka-go. <sup>15</sup> Wông-djong tsô-djü tao-cʽü di-min-teng, s-teh keh di-fông tu heh-en-go. Væn-pah boh-ts dzing-loh-tong din-li-go su-tsʽæ, jü-li-go ko-ts, long-tsong kyʽüoh-de: s-teh læ ʽen Yiæ-gyih koh, jü-li teng din-li-go su-tsʽæ tsʽing-tsʽing-go tong-si ih-ngæn m-neh dzing-loh. <sup>16</sup> Fah-lao ziu kwʽa-kwʽa dziao-long Mo-si Üô-leng, wô, Ngô z teh-ze ng-go Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô, yi teh-ze ng-lah. <sup>17</sup> Næn-kæn, tæn-tsih keh-tsao, tsʽing ng kwʽun-shü ngô ze, gyiu ng Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô, min. diao ngô keh-tsao-go si. <sup>18</sup> Gyi li-kʽæ Fah-lao tseo-cʽih, gyiu-gyiu Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>19</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô ziu fah-kyʽi do-do si-fong læ, pô wông-djong cʽü-tsing ʽOng-hæ, s-teh ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-ka m-neh ih-go wông-djong dzing-loh. <sup>20</sup> Dæn-z Yiæ-ʽo-wô ngang Fah-lao-go sing, feh-kʽeng fông Yi-seh-lih ts-seng kyʽi.

<sup>12</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「伸出手来<u>埃及</u>国，使得蝗虫走进<u>埃及</u>地界，吃箇地上百样蔬菜，就是雹子所剩落东个。」<sup>13</sup> <u>摩西</u>就提起拐杖来<u>埃及</u>国。耶和华使得东风整日整夜吹箇地方。到天亮东风带勒蝗虫走进。<sup>14</sup> 蝗虫散开<u>埃及</u>遍地，停来<u>埃及</u>团圈地界里向。是顶厉害。从前呒有箇种介蝗虫，以后也再呒有箇种介个。<sup>15</sup> 蝗虫遮住到处地面顶，使得箇地方都黑暗个。凡百雹子剩落东田里个蔬菜、树里个果子，拢总吃了。使得来咸<u>埃及</u>国，树里等田里个蔬菜青青个东西一眼呒得剩落。<sup>16</sup> <u>法老</u>就快快召拢<u>摩西</u>、<u>亚伦</u>，话，「我是得罪尔个神明耶和华，又得罪尔拉。<sup>17</sup> 难间，单只箇遭，请尔宽恕我罪，求尔神明耶和华，免掉我箇遭个死。」<sup>18</sup> 其离开<u>法老</u>走出，求求耶和华。<sup>19</sup> 耶和华就发起大大西风来，把蝗虫吹进<u>红</u>海，使得咸<u>埃及</u>地界呒得一个蝗虫剩落。<sup>20</sup> 但是耶和华硬<u>法老</u>个心，弗肯放<u>以色列</u>子孙去。

<sup>21</sup> Yiæ-ʽo-wô teng Mo-si wô, Ng sing-cʽih siu læ dziao-leh tʽin, s-teh Yiæ-gyih koh <sup>\[1\]</sup>heh-en, tsih-hao læ heh-en li-hyiang moh-læ-moh-kyʽi. <sup>22</sup> Mo-si ziu sing-cʽih siu læ dziao-leh tʽin, ʽen Yiæ-gyih di-fông sæn-nyih kong-fu moh-tsʽeh-di-en. <sup>23</sup> Keh sæn-nyih li-hyiang nying min tu kʽen-feh-kyin, yia m-yiu nying bô-kyʽi li-kʽæ gyi deng-cʽü-go: tsih-yiu Yi-seh-lih ts-seng oh-li yiu liang-kwông. <sup>24</sup> Fah-lao eo Mo-si wô, Ng-lah kyʽi voh-z Yiæ-ʽo-wô: tæn-tsih liu ng-lah ngeo yiang læ-tong; ng-go siao-nying yia hao teng ng-lah dô-kô kyʽi. <sup>25</sup> Mo-si wô, Ng pih-iao do foh-li teng tsi-veh kao-fu ah-lah siu-li, hao kyʽi tsi ah-lah Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>26</sup> Ah-lah sang-kʽeo yia tsong iao dô-kô kyʽi; ih-tsah di-ts tu feh dzing-loh-tong: ing-we ah-lah iao læ gyi-lah cong-nyiang do-leh voh-z ah-lah Jing-ming Yiæ-ʽo-wô: ping-tsʽia feh-zing tao keh-deo z-ʽeo, wa feh-hyiao-teh kæ yüong soh-si voh-z Yiæ-ʽo-wô. <sup>27</sup> Dæn-z Yiæ-ʽo-wô ngang Fah-lao-go sing, feh-kʽeng fông gyi-lah kyʽi. <sup>28</sup> Fah-lao teng gyi wô, Ng li-kʽæ ngô kyʽi, zi yüong siao-sing, m-nao tsæ kyin ngô-go min; ing-we teng ngô kyin-min-go nyih-ts ng pih-ding iao si. <sup>29</sup> Mo-si wô, Ng shih-wô z-go; ngô feh tsæ kyin ng-go min.

<sup>21</sup> 耶和华等<u>摩西</u>话，「尔伸出手来朝勒天，使得<u>埃及</u>国黑暗，只好来黑暗里向摸来摸去<sup>\[1\]</sup>。」<sup>22</sup> <u>摩西</u>就伸出手来朝勒天，咸<u>埃及</u>地方三日工夫墨漆地暗。<sup>23</sup> 箇三日里向人面都看弗见，也呒有人爬起离开其庉处个。只有<u>以色列</u>子孙屋里有亮光。<sup>24</sup> <u>法老</u>讴<u>摩西</u>话，「尔拉去服侍耶和华。单只留尔拉牛羊来东。尔个小人也好等尔拉大家去。」<sup>25</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「尔必要驮福礼等祭物交付阿拉手里，好去祭阿拉神明耶和华。<sup>26</sup> 阿拉牲口也总要大家去。一只蹄子都弗剩落东。因为阿拉要来其拉中央驮勒服侍阿拉神明耶和华。并且弗曾到箇头时候，还弗晓得该用啥西服侍耶和华。」<sup>27</sup> 但是耶和华硬<u>法老</u>个心，弗肯放其拉去。<sup>28</sup> <u>法老</u>等其话，「尔离开我去，自用小心，呒得好再见我个面。因为等我见面个日子尔必定要死。」<sup>29</sup> <u>摩西</u>话，「尔说话是个。我弗再见尔个面。」

<sup>\[1\] *ʽÔh wô*, Keh heh-en ʽeo-ʽeo jih-jih, tsing-ziang moh-leh-djoh go. 或话，箇黑暗厚厚实实，正像摸勒着个。（原注）</sup>
